## Plan corrections (canon contradicts the plan)

1. **The regulars do not go inland.** Only the marines raid inland under Keneb. Keneb counts "the Adjunct and her regular infantry led by [Fist] Blistig, the Khundryl Burned Tears, and the Perish" as separate parts of the invasion (W17; T p. 501–502, "regulars", "Blistig", "Khundryl", "Perish" all hit there) [H]. Blistig is with the Adjunct at Second Maiden Fort (W14) and on the fleet up the river (W20, R24.1 S10) [H]. Keneb's camp is 671 soldiers (W21); with the forward squads, "under a thousand" (W22) [1]. So "the Third, regular infantry of Blistig's 9th, landing and marching inland" stands in no canon gap as written. **Two fixes (David's call):**
   - **(A) recommended:** the Third is lent to Keneb's column before the landing, as a squad of heavies. BH has the precedent: Hellian and Urb, City Garrison men, were reassigned into the marines (BH R5). It keeps every chapter of the plan. The Third is never called marines; "lent to the Fist's column".
   - **(B):** the Third stays with the Adjunct. Ch 1: the burning seen from the fleet and the landing at Second Maiden Fort. Ch 3: holding the fort among the Shake. Ch 5: the landing on the Lether River and the Adjunct's battle (the mortal fight). Ch 7: Letheras. Truer to Book Six's `adjunct` road ("out with the fleet"), but it rewrites Ch 3 and Ch 5 and puts the Third against the Letherii army, where the Letherii squad also stands.
2. **The battle before Letheras is two fights, not one** (W22; R24.1 S1, S9, S10) [H]:
   - **The killing field:** the marines form a circle on a cleared plain about a day from the city. Letherii mages on a ridge, watched by Sirryn Kanar and "a veteran Letherii commander", loose a ritual a week in the making, meant to kill the marines and their own Edur together. Beak's dome of silver fire holds it, and he dies. Hanradi Khalag's Edur shelter in the dome, then walk home, "renouncing empire" (W22).
   - **The Adjunct's battle:** the Letherii army then marches to the river to meet "a new landing—the Adjunct's main force" (W22). Her force shatters it, and she lets her soldiers kill the broken (R24.1 S9, S10). **It is told after the fact**: no canon map, no named Letherii units [1].
   - So the plan's "the main Malazan army comes by sea and fights the main Letherii army" is right for the second fight only. Beak's death is the first.
3. **Bowl, Lutes and Tavos Pond (Ch 5).** The plan has them die in ch 21 (R21). W21 doesn't say so, and T finds Lutes and Tavos Pond on p. 652 and Bowl on p. 624, after ch 21 begins. Open (§15 Q1). The hard limit stands either way.
4. **Tavore is never a viewpoint** in the series. The Ch 7 vision "Tavore before the city" goes through Lostara Yil (R24.1 S10).
5. **Brys has no viewpoint before ch 24.** The vision "Brys among the dead" becomes Bruthen Trana on the sea floor (W20; R24.2 S3).
6. **Ch 4's "the Awl strike the Edur"** is the Awl'dan war, a thousand leagues east and a hard limit. What reaches Letheras is news. The army left at home is thin: Bivatt's army is in the Awl'dan, and "five armies" went east against the Bolkando (R24.1 S24).
7. **Fiddler at the coliseum:** Fiddler's group reaches the arena after Rhulad is dead, and Trull is killed there (R24.2 S47–49) [1]. Hard limit as planned.
8. **The `army` road holds:** Brys, as King's Sword, takes "a detachment of the Letherii Army" with the Bonehunters into the Wastes, and Malazan sergeants train Letherii troops in Letheras first (WB, DoD ch 1, 3) [1].

## 1. Shape of the novel

- **Chapters:** 24, plus a Prologue and an Epilogue, in four books (W, contents) [1]:
  - The Emperor in Gold (ch 1–6);
  - Layers of the Dead (7–12);
  - Knuckles of the Soul (13–18);
  - Reaper's Gale (19–24).
- **The Malazans** come ashore at the end of ch 12. Yan Tovis sees "hundreds of ships burning", Malazan dromons, off Boaral Keep (W12) [1].
- **Six threads:**
  - Letheras: the palace, the Patriotists, Tehol;
  - the Awl'dan: Bivatt, Brohl Handar, Redmask, Toc;
  - the fugitives: Seren, Fear, Silchas Ruin, Udinaas, Kettle;
  - the Refugium: Trull, Onrack, Quick Ben, Hedge;
  - the Shake: Twilight, Yedan;
  - the Malazans.
  The Malazans meet the Letheras thread only in ch 24.

## 2. The Malazan thread (game Ch 1, 3, 5, 7)

- **ch 13: the landing (game Ch 1).**
  - Tavore's speech to her officers on the Froth Wolf: they follow the Edur fleet that massacred Sepik, and their future may be unwitnessed by the empire, but not by themselves (W13; T p. 357, 361, "unwitnessed") [H]. Lostara Yil and Banaschar fill the pay chests (W13) [1].
  - The marines land on the north-west coast. Their transports are burned by their commander, so there is no way back (W13; T p. 358, 360 hit "burned the transports") [H].
  - The squads reverse their sleep and move by night. Mages use Mockra for camouflage and to mask scent. Hellian salvages rum from a burning ship (W13) [1].
  - Keneb commands; he doubts the Adjunct and orders the march inland. Faradan Sort takes Beak as her tracker of hidden squads (W13) [1].
  - Second Maiden Fort, the Shake prison isle, is taken by an advance force: Balm's squad, and Cord's with Sinn. It will be the army's headquarters (W13) [1].
- **ch 14:**
  - Hellian's squad ambushes an Edur troop at **Boaral Keep** (W14).
  - Faradan Sort and Beak steal horses from a way station (W14).
  - Fiddler: "we need the support of the farmers and swineherds" (W14).
  - At Second Maiden Fort, Shurq Elalle tells Tavore the Letherii won't rise, and the ruling class will guard what it has (W14) [1].
- **ch 15:** an Edur and four Letherii ride into Letheras wounded by "powerful crossbows". The Chancellor has the Letherii survivors silenced (W15) [1].
- **ch 17: the night raids (game Ch 3).**
  - Sort notes the Letherii working with the Edur: the plan to free them "will not work" (W17).
  - Ambushes by Letherii crossbowmen and a mage. Sands is killed (W17).
  - **The Drum**, Fiddler's cusser trap on a road. **The Kenryll'ah demons** at a farmhouse kill Uru Hela (W17).
  - Thom Tissy counts ten smokes from a tree (W17) [1].
- **ch 18:** the Malazans maul a Letherii brigade, "the Borthen Brigade" (W18). T has no hit for "Borthen": the name is open (§15 Q3).
- **ch 21: the village (game Ch 5).**
  - The squads are funnelled between Edur bands to the north and the Lether River to the south (W21).
  - **Hellian and Urb's "liberation":** in every village they kill the officials, money-lenders and advocates, then let the people loot and flee. The people warn them in return (W21) [1].
  - Hundreds of Edur close on the tavern village. A house-to-house fight follows. A cusser levels the street and the Factor's house (W21) [1].
  - **The 3rd Company** under Badan Gruk and Primly arrive by stolen fisher boat on the river, "barely a day's march from Letheras" (W21) [1].
  - Keneb, with 671, orders a hard push for the capital. Faradan Sort commands the rearguard of ten squads of heavies (W21) [1].
- **ch 22: the killing field (game Ch 5's end and Ch 6).** See correction 2. Under a thousand marines are on the plain. Beak dies. Fiddler's silent truce with Hanradi Khalag. The road to Letheras is open (W22) [1].
- **ch 24 (game Ch 7)** [1], R only:
  - The marines build Beak's barrow; Letherii refugees flee the city (R24.1 S1).
  - Sirryn Kanar flees back into the city by a postern door, and "the mob" controls much of it (S7).
  - **Keneb sends the marines through the tunnel, takes the gate himself, and sends Sort and Fiddler for the palace** (R24.2 S1).
  - Fiddler's and Gesler's squads destroy a City Garrison detachment with munitions (S16–18).
  - Icarium's machine sets white fire across the city (S20–23).
  - Fiddler, Hedge and Quick Ben drive off **Silchas Ruin** in dragon form (S39).
  - **The Letherii garrison surrenders to Tavore.** The crowd shouts "Saviour" (Brys) and "Emperor" (Tehol) (S43).
  - Gnol is killed and his men surrender. Varat Taun leads the Malazans to the arena (S45–47).
  - **Sirryn Kanar kills Trull** over Rhulad's body; Koryk shoots Sirryn, and Quick Ben sends him to Hood (S48–50).
- **Epilogue:** Tehol has been emperor three weeks. Tavore is expected to announce the army's departure. Varat Taun is Preda, rebuilding the city (WE) [1].

## 3. The Letherii thread (game Ch 2, 4, 6)

- **The Patriotists:**
  - Letheras HQ: Invigilator **Karos Invictad**, his aide **Tanal Yathvanar**; 320 detained in one week, a third for the Drownings (W1) [1].
  - Its charter is "the loyal and the disloyal", with no line between Edur and Letherii (W1).
  - Agents at the market gates in Drene; **Orbyn Truthfinder** heads it there (W2).
  - **Sirryn Kanar** is a lieutenant of its Palace Cell (W9) [1].

  - Chancellor **Triban Gnol** keeps the Emperor isolated. **Bruthen Trana**, the Edur liaison to the Patriotists, is turned away (W3, W5, W7) [H].

- **Who the Edur trust:** Tomad and Uruth are jailed under Gnol (W15), and drown in their cells by "clerical error" (W18). Mosag kills Bruthen Trana to sanctify a temple (W15) [1].
- **The army:**

- **Where the Edur go:** after the killing field, Hanradi leads his Edur home (W22). In the city "all the Edur have left" (R24.1 S7, S21). Mosag "sent his people home" (R24.2 S9) [H].

- **The end of the regime** (all R24.2) [1]:

## 8. The Rising (the book's system)

- **Tavore's plan:** the enemy is the Edur, not the Letherii, and the Malazans "encourage a general uprising" (W14) [1].
- **Canon's answer:** Shurq says the Letherii won't rise (W14), and Sort sees them fighting beside the Edur (W17) [H]. **So the Rising starts low and is earned.**
- **The Indebted:**
  - Hellian's villages: officials and money-lenders killed, the people warning the marines (W21).
  - Venitt Sathad, an Indebted, kills Factor Letur Anict amid Drene's riots (W19, W22).
  - In Drene the poor fight the garrison and storm the Factor's estate; Orbyn is told the Patriotists "must be sacrificed to the mob" (W19).
  - Feather Witch's cult grows among slaves and the Indebted (W20).
- **The Rat Catchers:** outlawed (W3). Rucket and Ormly fund Tehol (fifty thousand coins, W3), pay shouters in the crowd, and make Tehol a hero to the people (R24.2 S10, S42) [H].
- **Tehol's scheme:**
  - Cracks in the foundation, and sixteen lacquered tiles from Onyx (W3).
  - Bugg's endless loans to pay interest on loans (W18).
  - The coin vanishes; the economy collapses (W19).
  - Janath in a cell sees the Patriotists making Tehol a martyr (W20) [1].
- **In the city's last day** the mob holds the streets, the refugees fear the city more than the invaders (R24.1 S1, S7), and the garrison surrenders (R24.2 S43).
