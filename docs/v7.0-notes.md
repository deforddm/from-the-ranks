## Nerve and Dust (every number)

**Nerve** (`S.nerve`, 0..10, start **5**) is how steady the raw company around the squad is.
- **`b4Nerve(d, why)`** changes it, clamps it, notes it ("Why: Nerve +1.") and tallies `nerveUp`/`nerveDown`. The chapters lower it (the omen, the news, the raids) and raise it (the squad's steadiness, the Khundryl).
- The engine raises it itself once: **+1** when a fight with `def.company` is won with nobody in the squad down.
- `b4NerveNow()`, and **`b4Mood()`**: 'steady' at 7+, 'shaky' at 3 or less, '' otherwise.
- **`nerveHTML(road)`** is the widget for a node's `html` (two bars, Book Two's chain box).
- The HUD pill (`#chainp`) shows "Nerve n", and "Dust n" once there is dust.

**The company regulars.** A fight with **`def.company = {at:[[x, y], …], n:1}`** (or `true`) has **n** regulars of the 9th beside the squad (`FOES.b4_regular`, "Ninth regular", hp 15 + 3 a level, ac 13 (+ the level's armour), atk 4 + level, 1d8+1 (+ the level's damage), move 4, drawn as Book Two's soldier).
- The default `n` is 1, placed at the `at` tiles (or beside the squad's first tile).
- **Nerve 7+: one more; 9+: two more.**
- **Nerve 3 or less:** at the end of round 2 each regular breaks and leaves the field, with a chance of **25% for each point of Nerve below 4** (Nerve 3 25%, 2 50%, 1 75%, 0 100%).
- The fight's log says which: steady, a regular, or "may not stay". `S.f.b4_broke` and the tally `b4Broke` count the ones who ran.

**Dust** (`S.dust`, 0..3) is the Whirlwind's sand.
- Set at `startChapter(n)` from **`B4DUST = {1:0, 2:0, 3:0, 4:0, 5:1, 6:2, 7:3}`**. A chapter may set `S.dust` itself after that, for one stretch, and **`def.dust`** overrides it for one fight. `b4DustNow()` is the dust in force.
- Each point: **every shot's range −1, never below 2.** This counts squad, allies and enemies (`rng > 1`).
- **Sorcery sputters at 5% per point** (5%, 10%, 15%; `b4SputterPct()`):
  - A squadmate's strain-costing spell comes out at **half effect** (Book Two's fouled-warren mechanism). The log says it; `S.f.b4_sputters` and the tally `b4Sputters` count it.
  - An enemy with a sorcery trick (`SORC`: drain, shadowstep, howl, daze, darkness, mark, sbolt) loses its sorcery for that turn and fights with what it carries.
- **Never sputters:** spirit-craft (`a.spirit`), Elder tricks (`a.elder`), anything under the Leper card, and anything under the grandmother's boon.
- The log names the dust at the start of every fight with dust.

**Also as Book Three has them**, under `b4_` flags:
- `def.away` (fought without them);
- `def.hurtMost` (`S.f.b4_hurtMost`; never the sergeant or a recruit);
- `def.lastStand` (`S.f.b4_lastHeld` 1/0, `S.f.b4_lastRounds`; no god, no retry, on to `def.after`);
- every fight writes `S.f.b4_lastRoundsFought`.
- Mortal fights (`mortal:true`, `kill(id)`) work as everywhere.
- The losing banner says "The squad is down".
