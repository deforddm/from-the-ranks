# Book Four's games at the fires (src/79_b4_games.js)

Five games, one at each chapter's fire, on the tables' frame (36: `mgOpen`, `mgPanel`, `mgKeys`, `MG.anim`), built the way Book Three's are (69, docs/v6.13-notes.md). The chapters' fire nodes are wrapped from 79 (`wrap`, `after`): each game's choice goes in right after "Talk with the squad.", so the fire keeps its rest, its talk, the swap, the pipe, the ways on and "Not now", and its `again:` line is untouched. Each game's after-node is added to the chapter's `dlg`, ending "Back to the fire.". No chapter file is changed.

**Keth leads the feather** on the Book Two `wickans` road when he walks with the squad (`b4FeatherLead()`: `b4In('warlock') && b4Was('b2_c7_key') === 'wickans'`): the fire's choice and the game's first word are his, and the grandmother's.

**Pay** is in `b4ShellsPay`, `b4CupPay`, `b4HandsPay`, `b4FusePay`, `b4FeatherPay`, each guarded by `S.f.b4_<game>Paid`, never in a node's `fx`. A game left early (`MG.leave`) pays nothing.

**Deeds** (`S.deeds`): `b4ShellsWon/Lost/Call/Palm`, `b4CupWon/Lost`, `b4HandsWon/Lost/Even`, `b4FuseWon/Lost/Clean`, `b4FeatherWon/Lost`, `b4Feathers` (feathers taken). The Deeds page lists the five as "At the fires" under Nerve and Dust (`b4DeedsExtra`, wrapped).

**Journal:** each chapter's `journal()` is wrapped to add the game's line once it has been played to the end.

**Checks inside a game** go through `b4gRoll(stat, dc, who, edges)`: the engine's `check()` with the scene's named edges, tallied like a choice's check; the game reads the tier and pays its own "yes, but" (never silver except in Shells, where money is the scene, and even there the cost is the game folding).

## Claude's calls
- The hustler is unnamed ("the Aren man"), as the plan asked; his figure is `hissari_m`.
- The cup's Khundryl are unnamed riders. Nothing explains the black tears; Gall's family is not mentioned; Imra is not in it; Orvas does not quote Gall's oath. Orvas warns against boasting of the Chain, and none of the boasts touch it.
- The Seti archers at the hand game are two unnamed women (one with her leg strapped to a board, one with a bandaged head), not Ch4's archer with the bound left hand, who could not play it. The hand game as a Seti game is an invention.
- The company's sapper is an unnamed woman of the Second Company.
- The feather is ridden behind the horse lines (Ch6's own horse lines and "the Wickan youth at the horse lines"); "some forty" youths watch. Temul is named only as the youths' captain and is not in the scene.
