## Two squads, one save

- **Threads:** `S.thread` is `'t'` (the Third, with the Bonehunters' heavies) or `'l'` (the Letherii, the Sixth Company in Brys's escort). `B9_CH = {1:'t', 2:'l', 3:'t', 4:'l', 5:'t', 6:'l', 7:'t'}`; `startChapter(n)` calls `b9Use(B9_CH[n])` first, then the march (below).
- **What each squad keeps** (`B9_KEYS`): Book Seven's list plus `injury` (the v10.2 wound slot) and `b9r` (its Rations). The waiting squad lives in `S.th[other]`. Shared: `S.f`, `S.stats`, `S.chapter`, `S.chapters`, `S.diff`, `S.b9` (Witness).
- **`b9Use(t)`** swaps, sets `TPL.sgt` from `BOOKSNAP.hoc.sgt` (the Third) or `BOOKSNAP.mt.sgt` (the Letherii) **with Book Nine's own epithet, history and quest on top** (`B9_SGT`), clears the portrait cache; idempotent. `setBook('dod')` re-applies the sergeant.
- **`b9As(fn)`** runs `fn(thread)` with `S.book` set to the squad's own earlier book (`'hoc'` or `'mt'`) for a moment, synchronously. **The v10.0.2 rule is built in:** `fn` gets the squad read before the switch, and `b9Thread()` still answers correctly inside (`b9AsT`). `b9On()` is false inside, on purpose.
- **Helpers:** `b9On()`, `b9Thread()`, `b9Other()`, `b9L()`, `b9Third()`, `b9Unit(t)`, `b9In(id)`, `b9OtherName()`, `b9Coin(n)` (docks for the Letherii, silver for the Third), `b9SgtLook()`, `b9ChCount()`, `b9Key(n)`, `b9LethRoad()`. `B9_TH[t]`: `book, look, unit, squad, army, who, core, fresh, recruits, lvl, cap`.

## Rations (plan, settled 2; the main track)

- **Each squad's own food and water, 0–10**, in its half (`S.b9r = {n, lo, hi}`), so the Letherii's supply train and the Bonehunters' wagons are counted apart (Claude's call). **Both start at 6** ("the march ration": the plan's "starts mid").
- **Tiers** (`b9RatTier()`, words `B9_RATW`): `starving` 0 ("nothing in the wagons"), `hungry` 1–2 ("half a ration and less"), `short` 3–4 ("short rations"), `fed` 5–7 ("the march ration"), `full` 8–10 ("full rations"). `b9Hungry()` is hungry or starving.
- `b9Ration(n, why)` moves it (noted with the reason and any tier change, clamped, tallied `b9RatUp` / `b9RatDown`, HUD updated, saved; returns the new value). `b9Rations()` reads it.
- **The engine's own move, the march:** at the start of Chapters 3, 4 and 5 the playing squad loses 2; at 6 and 7 it loses 1 (once a chapter, flags `b9_march3`…`b9_march7`). Chapters 1 and 2 are in Letheras. (`B9_MARCH`.)
- **In a fight:** `short` −5% maximum health for every squadmate, `hungry` −10%, `starving` −15% (allies are fed by their own wagons). The log says so at the start.
- **On checks:** a check with `rations:true` gets the edge **"Rations"**: full +1, fed 0, short −1, hungry −2, starving −3. Any other check, while hungry or starving, gets **"An empty belly"** −1.
- **Shown:** the HUD pill "Rations N" (red at 4 or less), `rationsHTML()` for a node (with the Witness line once there is one), the journal's first line, the count (now, lowest, found, eaten, Witness kept), Deeds' "Rations" section.

## Witness (plan, settled 2: "a little")

- **One beat in each of Chapters 3 to 7** (`B9WIT = {3:'t', 4:'l', 5:'t', 6:'l', 7:'t'}`), in the chapter's key scene or at its fire: does the squad still march for something (`'kept'`: each other, the Adjunct's word, their own witness) or because the column does (`'doubt'`)?
- `b9Wit(v, why, n = S.chapter)`: records the beat once (the first answer stands), noted, tallied `b9Kept` / `b9Doubt`. Returns null outside a beat chapter.
- `b9Witness(t)`: `{kept, doubt}` for a squad. `b9Faith(t)`: `'kept'` (kept ≥ doubt and at least one kept), `'doubt'` (doubt > kept), or `null` before any beat.
- A check with `witness:true` gets **"Our own witness"**: +1 on `kept`, −1 on `doubt`.
- **Read by:** Ch 6's road (`b9Faith('l')`) and Ch 7's key and finale (`b9Faith('t')`). Light by design: no HUD pill.
