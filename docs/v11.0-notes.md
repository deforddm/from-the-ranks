## The dark-warren pieces (v10.2 spec §2) and the jug

- **The Hood-touched charm** (`b8_hoodcharm`, trinket, role `any`). Once a fight, the first blow that takes its wearer from above half health to half or below is taken back off them and laid on the nearest enemy already at half or below (no roll, no soak, no ward). With no such enemy it waits. **Cold in Chapter 6** (`B8_CHARM_COLD`: Hood has fallen; it logs once and does nothing). A Ch 1–5 find while Hood stands; the writers place it.
- **The Kurald Galain shroud** (`b8_shroud`, "A Fold of Night"; trick, `use:'fight'`, elder, sorcery). Once a fight, the bearer or a squadmate within 4 (+`lvT()`) gets a ward of a full 15% (`wardCap`) at once, set directly: not a heal, so `healW` doesn't come into it. Never stacks (a bigger ward stays). **Free** (v10.3: it doesn't strike; the one-free-trick-a-turn limit applies). A Black Coral trick, Ch 3.
- **The jug of kelyk** (`b8_kelykjug`, trinket, role `any`; plan, 9:03 PM PST). Worn, it gives the action **Drink the kelyk** (`AB.b8_drink`, free, the wearer's first turn of a fight only): heal 2d6+`lvB()` (plain heal, no ward) and **+2 to hit for the fight** (carried into the second area). Afterwards the drinker carries **the kelyk-sickness** (`INJURIES.kelyk`, −1 to hit, the weakest wound: a real wound replaces it) until the next fire. A bad fall never rolls it (`giveInjury` wrapped). Words short and sour.
