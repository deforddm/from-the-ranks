# From the Ranks v6.10.0: the K'ell Hunter, and a rope man

Sun Oct 18, 2026. David's ask: with Vell in the party, the Grey Swords' approach about the K'ell should recognise him with dialogue and a bonus on the rope skill; and the fight should be much harder, as the books have it (K'ell Hunters nearly impossible for conventional mortal forces; brought down by skilled, coordinated opponents at great cost; resistant to sharpers, vulnerable to cussers).

## What changed
- `FOES.b3_khell`: hp 60→96, ac 15→17, atk 6→8, dmg 2d8+3→2d10+4, two attacks a turn, mv 7, init 4, `sk:['reach','parry']`, `dr:5`, `resist:{sharper:.5}`, `weak:{cusser:1.5}`. The roped one in the gully (`b3_c4_roped`): hp 40→60, ac 13, atk 7, dmg 2d8+3, two attacks, `dr:5`, parry, the same resist and weak.
- Engine: `u.dr` (damage a hide takes off an ordinary blow, never below 1; a critical ignores it) in `attack()`; `u.resist[kind]` / `u.weak[kind]` multipliers in `blast()`, the kind from `B.blastKind`, which `useAb` sets from the ability's item (the quorl drop counts as a sharper). The log says "rattles off" or "torn open" when they apply.
- Vell: see `docs/moi-continuity.md` (v6.10.0).

## The measure
Naive AI (the enemy AI driving the squad, no munitions, no tricks, no heals, level 11 with two Grey Sword allies), the whole hunt: Soldier 9/10 won, most wins with two or three squadmates down; Bridgeburner 1/6 won. The old numbers were 8/8 on Soldier with the squad untouched. Smart play has what the naive bot does not: cussers at ×1.5, the Wolves' Rope (−8 armour and −4 to hit on a boss for a round), Vell's rope, Rally, Ohl's hands, flanks, and a god if it comes to that. That is the pitch asked for: possible, at a cost.

## Tests
`tools/khell_test.mjs` (13 checks): the numbers, the hide on a blow, a sharper at half and a cusser at half again (a squadmate in the same blast unaffected), Vell's lines present with him and absent without, his +3 edge, his hook roping the Hunter in the open and not in the gully, the coil and the loyalty.
