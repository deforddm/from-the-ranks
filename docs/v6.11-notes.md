# From the Ranks v6.11.0: the Deck on a table

Sun Oct 18, 2026. David's ask: put some work into the Deck readings; improve the look of the reading; different backdrops for the cards to be dealt on; brainstorm first. His picks from the brainstorm: a table for every place, one per chapter across the three books; no reader's hands this pass; all the smaller pieces (a deck back per reader, the House's light on the table, a way in and out, the card panel, refused cards showing a face); and a spread per reader in this version.

## What changed
- `src/21b_reading_tables.js` (new). `READTABLE`, keyed `book:chapter` (`gotm:0` is the prologue, `gotm:6self` Tuft's own draw in the garden, `moi:7` resolves by the road to `moi:7a` or `moi:7b`): 24 tables, each `{surf, p, light, lp, lc, props, air, dark}`. Surfaces: `ground` (grit, stones, grass, leaves, ash, ruts, cracks), `cloth` (a laid sheet or the whole table; stripe, diamond or Moranth hex pattern; folds, wear, stains), `wood` (planks, a board inset with straps, ring stains), `lid` (a cask lid), `wheel` (a cart-wheel), `stone` (a flat stone, flags, a roof of slates, a stair), `hide` (pegged, with the hair), `shield` (hollow side up: the boss, the straps), `sack`. Lights: lantern, lamp, fire, torch, coals, the Spawn, dawn, dusk, noon (with the cart's shade), green dapple, paper lanterns, ridge fires, the forge of a burning city. Air: dust, ash, sparks, mist, leaves. Props laid about (a lantern, cups, a jug, a candle, a knife, a copper, a rope, stakes, cedar, a fallen paper moon, the bone cup, a pouch, sacking, a prayer-cloth, a wash-tub, a helm, a shoring beam, a barrow-stone), and each reader's kit by the deck.
- The surface and the props are painted once into an offscreen canvas (`makeReadingTable`, again on resize); every frame draws it, then the light (a multiply vignette from the light's place with its flicker, plus the kind's own glows), the air, and after the turn the House's light (`TBL_HOUSE`: Death's frost from the corners and a cold cast; Dark dims; Shadow gutters and goes violet; Life's green; Chains' slow red pulse; Light brightens; an Unaligned flare that decays). A frame costs under a millisecond in the headless browser.
- `DECKBACK`: a back per reader. 20's `drawCard` paints `DECKBACK[DECKBACK_NOW]` for a face-down card when the reader is not Tuft (Tuft's wheel stays as it was). `cardSequence` sets `DECKBACK_NOW` for the reading and puts it back to `tuft` on the way out; `inlineCard` sets it round its own flip. Marrow: ivory temple deck from Nap, worn corners, Soliel's lamp. Yedda: thin wood, bright red and green paint, a rope knot. The old Kanese: faded red with the prayer-cloth lattice and a sign. Benna: greasy, water-ringed, a soldier's stamp. Sohra: the card the bones make.
- `SPREADS` and `READER_SPREAD`: `row3` (Tuft), `cross5` (Marrow; four refuse, smaller), `rope3` (Yedda, paid out like a line), `fan3` (the Kanese), `askew3` (Benna, with a copper on the table), `ring` (Sohra: five knucklebones thrown from the cup into the smoke, settling in a ring, gathered and thrown twice more, and the card rising face up out of the smoke at 4.6 s; the captions are the bones' own). A slot is `{dx, yy, dy, rot, sc, flip}` in card widths and heights; the answer slot is `ans`. The refusers past the Crown, Sceptre and Orb are the Obelisk, the Knight and Oponn when they are not the card.
- `src/21`: the sequence over the table. Same timings as before (shuffle to 1.4 s, the spread from 1.5 s, the refusals at 2.9 and 3.9 s, the turn at 4.9 s, the words at 5.6 s, a tap or Back hurries to 6.2 s). A refused card shows its face for a full second before the dark cracks through it (a line in the card's hue, then the smoke). `#cardfx` fades in (`.in`) and out (550 ms; 250 ms into a vision; none under reduced motion) with the page behind already drawn; `AUDIO.setScene('dark')` for the reading and the scene it found given back on the way out (the Fold's second reading hands it on through `opt.scene`). The risen card tilts under a pointer held down (a skew and a moving glare) and settles when let go; a drag is not a tap. The House's mark (`TBL_SIGIL`) sits faint behind the words. With a vision in the card its glow breathes and motes rise off it. The text panel's dark is the table's (`--tbl` on `#cardfx`). Captions are brighter with a shadow, for lit tables.
- `backTop` (90): Back during a reading hurries it like a tap; once the words are up (`#cardfx[data-shown]`) it presses "Put the cards away". The phone's Back still never leaves the game.

## Found by looking
- The shield's strap and grip read as a face at phone size: now a boss hollow in the middle and two straight riveted straps.
- The knucklebones came out as rounded squares: a `drawBone` already existed in `36_minigames.js` (the bones game) and, the game being one scope, the later declaration won. Renamed `drawKnuckle`. Playbook rule: grep a new global name across `src/` before using it.
- The smoke under `screen` blending was invisible on a tan hide; plain soft glows from the cup toward the ring's centre now.
