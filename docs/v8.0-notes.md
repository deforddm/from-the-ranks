## Debt (every number)

Every soldier carries a debt in docks, **`S.debt[id]`**; **`S.debt0[id]`** is what they owed at the march (or when they joined).

- **`b5Debt(id)`**, **`b5DebtStart(id)`**, **`b5DebtTotal()`** (the living, walking or at camp), **`b5Debtors()`**.
- **`b5Owe(id, n, why)`**: they owe n more. Noted ("Why: Thegg owes 80 docks more."), tallied `b5Owed`.
- **`b5Pay(id, n, why)`**: the squad pays down up to n out of its own docks (`S.silver`), no more than it has or than is owed. Returns what was paid (0 with a note if nothing). Tallied `b5Paid`.
- **`b5Forgive(id, n, why)`**: the debt shrinks without the squad's docks (a bounty, a burned ledger, a dead holder). Tallied `b5Forgiven`.
- **`b5Standing(id)`**, against `S.debt0`:

| Standing | When | Crossing into it |
|---|---|---|
| `free` | nothing owed | loyalty +1, once (`S.f.b5_freed_<id>`; tally `b5Freed`) |
| `easing` | half the start or less | loyalty +1, once (`S.f.b5_eased_<id>`) |
| `held` | between | — |
| `pressed` | more than 1.25 × the start | loyalty −1, once (`S.f.b5_pressed_<id>`); the collectors: `S.f.b5_collectors` +1, tally `b5Pressed` |
| `drowning` | 1.5 × the start or more | `S.f.b5_drownRisk`; in Letheras (Chapters 1 and 7) **`b5DrowningThreat()`** is true |

  A soldier who started at nothing is `pressed` from 50 docks and `drowning` from 100. The sergeant has no loyalty to move.
- **`b5Collectors()`**: the debtors who are `pressed` or `drowning` (for a scene with a collector's men: foes `b5_bully`, `b5_collector`).
- **The dead's debts.** When `kill(id)` takes a soldier in Book Five, their debt passes to the heirs with the lost kit, as the army's ledger says (mt-canon §2): `S.f.b5_heirs[id] = docks`, noted once, off the squad's total, and **never charged to the squad**.
- **Shown**: the squad sheet (a Debt section on both tabs), the journal's first note, the HUD pill ("Debt n", and "Collectors n" when any), the pack, the count, Deeds ("Debt"), and **`debtHTML(road)`** for a node's `html` (Book Two's chain box, a bar per soldier).
- A recruit brings their own debt when they join (`b5DebtInit`).
- **The pay desk: `b5PayChoices(back, here?)`** returns a node's choices: for each debtor who owes, while the squad has docks, "10 docks on X's debt." (or what there is, if less) and "All we can on X's debt." (when that is more than 10), each through `b5Pay` and back to the desk (`here`, or the node showing it); then "Not now." to `back`. Each chapter's desk is `b5_c<n>_pay: () => ({sp, txt, html:debtHTML('…'), ch:b5PayChoices('b5_c<n>_fire')})`; add any chapter-only choice before it.

## Disarmed and rearmed (Chapter 4 `hold`, Chapter 5)

- **`b5Disarm()`**: every `weapon`-slot item comes off every soldier (walking or at camp) and out of `S.kit` into **`S.f.b5_stash`** (ids; `S.f.b5_stashWho` remembers who wore each), and **`S.f.b5_disarmed = 1`**. Armour and trinkets stay. Disarmed, the squad fights with its base attacks: the knives and staves the Edur let them keep. Noted once ("Swords, spears and bows go on the Edur's pile … Knives, shields and paybooks stay."). Idempotent: a second call only sweeps up a weapon gained since. Returns how many it took.
- **`b5Rearm(fallback)`**: only while disarmed. Clears `b5_disarmed`, sets **`S.f.b5_rearmed = 1`**, and gives back the stash, each piece to whoever wore it if they walk and the slot is free, else where it suits best (`placeGear`). If the stash is empty (a fresh `--from=5` save, or nothing was carried), it gives the `fallback` ids that exist in `ITEMS` instead (Chapter 5: `b5Rearm(['b5_c5_cacheblade', 'b5_c5_cachebow'])`). Returns the ids given; a second call gives nothing.
