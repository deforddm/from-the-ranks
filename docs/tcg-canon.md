## Plan corrections (the gate: does a planned beat contradict canon?)

**Yes, three (1, 2, 3); the rest are small.** Each fix has a recommendation first.

### 1. There is no pass on the Letherii's road. The pass is Paran's (Ch 4, Ch 6)

- **Canon's pass:** "a mountain pass blocked by Forkrul Assail and the Perish from the fleet". Paran's Host comes to it from the North Citadel; Kalam, with Quick Ben's help, kills Brother Serenity there by night; next day Paran's sappers, with Moranth munitions he took from a Moranth warehouse through the Deck, "massacre the Forkrul Shriven army"; the Perish surrender (WC22; WT ch 22) [H]. No source names the pass [O].
- **The southern allies' road:** the Bonehunters and the Khundryl cross the desert "alone"; the allies "skirt the desert to the south and head for Kolanse City" (WC7; WT ch 7) [H]. On the march the Letherii are in poor shape, trying to keep pace with the Bolkando, who are trying to keep up with the Perish (R13 S1) [1]. **No mountains or pass on that road were found** in any source [O].
- **Where the Letherii really fight:** the allied attack on "the approaches to Kolanse City", a feint to hold the Forkrul army while the Che'Malle and the T'lan Imass take the Spire (WC23; R23.2) [H]. The Perish hold the Kolansii centre at first, then turn on the Assail (WC23; R23.2 S2) [H]. Fighting is round "the Kolansii trenches", the Perish's position "on the bank", and the Spire (R23.2 S3–S4, S14–S19) [1].
- **Fix (A), recommended: the Letherii's mortal fight is the allied battle before Kolanse City (ch 23),** on a stretch of the Letherii line no source names. Never the Perish centre, the trenches the Saphii storm, Brys's ride, the Voice, the seawater cloud, the Gilk and Teblor block, or Grub's relief (§4). Ch 4 becomes the long southern march (hard going, the Perish pulling ahead); "the mountains" go.
- **Fix (B):** keep "the pass" as the game's own ground: a defile on the southern march where a Kolansii outpost holds the road, a fight canon never mentions. It costs nothing in canon, but it puts the squad's last fight outside the book's climax, and players who know TCG will look for Paran's pass in it.

### 2. The Third's mortal fight cannot stand on Fiddler's hill (Ch 7)

- **What the crossing ends in:** out of the desert the army marches into Kolanse and meets the northern Assail army (W *Tavore Paran*) [1]. On the day, Tavore takes the regulars west with the Khundryl and the T'lan Imass; **Fiddler takes "the Marines and Heavies" back to a hill** where the Crippled God's body will lie (WC24; W *Tavore Paran*: "barely a hundred strong") [H]. The Forkrul split their army in three: the largest at the regulars, the others at Fiddler and at Paran (WC24) [1].
- **The hill is canon's most named ground in the series:** Shortnose and Corabb die defending the god's body; Hedge's last cusser is a dud; Fiddler is down to "about twenty marines"; Cuttle dies; Quick Ben and Kalam arrive; D'rek's ring; the dragons; Cotillion (R24.3 S38–S54; R24.4 S1–S9) [H with WC24]. "Only a handful" are left (WC24) [1].
- **The regulars' field** is a large battle with stretches no one names: "the Bonehunters were cut down in their thousands" (W *Tavore Paran*); "a few hundred" left at the worst (WC24) [H]. Its named scenes: Gall and Hanavat; Faradan Sort's warning and her charge behind the Khundryl; Brother Aloft's Voice on Tavore, Lostara, Ruthan Gudd and Henar; Badalle and the Shards; the regulars Ordinary Grey, Grid Ffan, Could Howl and Hare Ravage leaving the line for the Adjunct; Gall's death; Blistig, Sort and Kindly fighting on (R24.3 S1–S28) [1]. **It ends** when Paran reaches a ridge, plays a card, and Mathok charges through a portal with thousands; the Kolansii drop to their knees (R24.3 S29–S38; W *Tavore Paran*, ch 24, UK HB p. 871: "relieved by the forces of her brother") [H].
- **Fix (A), recommended: the Third's mortal fight is a stretch of the regulars' line,** at the far end from the Adjunct and the Khundryl, held until Paran's relief comes over the ridge and the Kolansii kneel. **Claude's call, marked so:** on the last nights of the crossing the Third was lent to a regulars' company as file-closers (or left in the wagon traces, R17 S1, S7), and the order for the hill never reached it. Canon never counts the heavies on the hill, so one squad elsewhere changes no outcome. It is the book's biggest margin; the brief should say it once, plainly, and never again.
- **Fix (B):** the Third's mortal fight is earlier, at the desert's edge on the last night: a Kolansii picket at the first water, ground and fight both the game's own. The battle day is then seen from the regulars' rear, not mortal. Safer still, but the series' last mortal fight would be a skirmish.
- **Rejected:** the hill's lower slope. Every survivor there is one of canon's "handful", and the squad would stand under the chaining.

### 3. The Fourth cannot land on the Kolanse coast (Ch 7)

- **The coast is the enemy's.** The Perish fleet, "ships of war … grey as wolves upon the water", lands on the Forkrul Assail's shore to serve them (GR5) [F]; the Perish "from the fleet" hold Paran's pass for the Assail (WC22) [1]. The Spire's harbour holds "Forkrul Assail, Kolansii, and Perish warships" until Hood freezes it (W *Hood*; WC23: Perish Thrones of War sink in Kolanse Bay) [H]. A lone ship of veterans would land into the Assail.
- **Paran's Host did not come by sea.** It appears "from nowhere" in a keep in Assail country: Paran opened a warren from Aren (GR5) [F]; the Host took the North Citadel, broke the Assail's siege, and went south (W *North Citadel*; WC21) [H for the citadel, F for Aren]. It is the Host of Book One (Onearm's Host under Paran since BH, series-canon §(c)).
- **The one canon landing** is King Tehol's: "supplies and reinforcements … by ship, including Teblor", reported by messengers from the coast to the southern allies (WC21; WT ch 21; GR21) [H]. Where they land: [O].
- **Fix (A), recommended: the Fourth reaches Seven Cities, finds its old Host at Aren, and goes with it** (veterans with time-served papers, taken on as the Host's own old soldiers). The gate, the North Citadel and the pass are told after, in one page of their words, headline only (rule 109). **They come over the ridge with Paran's relief** onto the Third's stretch of the regulars' field, the turn of the Third's mortal fight, and join in Ch 7. Book Eight's "Somewhere east and over the sea … nobody agrees which or where" already allows it; write no compass word for the voyage. **Timing is open** [O]: the Fourth sails at TtH's end; the Host is already in Kolanse by TCG ch 5. Never print a count of weeks.
- **Fix (B):** the Fourth comes to Letheras, hears the Malazans have gone east, and sails with Tehol's supply ships to the coast by the southern allies (ch 21). That is a real Kolanse landing, but it meets the Letherii's column, not the Third: on the default road the Fourth joins the Third only after the fighting, at the barrows (Ch 7's last phase).
- **On a Third `south` road** (with Brys), (A) meets the Third at the barrows after the battles; (B) meets it the day before the allied battle. Either way the Fourth never fights beside the Letherii squad unless the brief says so.

### 4. The Letherii may cross the desert: Henar Vygulf did (`adjunct` road)

- WC7 has the Bonehunters and Khundryl crossing "alone", but **Henar Vygulf, a Letherii officer, is with the Bonehunters at the end**: Tavore's last charge is "accompanied by Ruthan Gudd, Lostara Yil and Henar Vygulf" (W *Tavore Paran*, ch 24, UK HB p. 860); Aloft's Voice flays him (R24.3 S8–S13) [H].
- **Fix:** a Book Nine `adjunct` Letherii squad crosses with the Malazans as a few Letherii who stayed (never beside Henar). Its Ch 2 and 4 are in the crossing behind the Third's company; its Ch 6 mortal fight is the **morning** of the regulars' battle on its own stretch, and the Third's Ch 7 is the afternoon and the relief (Book Nine's pattern of one battle across Ch 6–7). The two never stand on one stretch unless the brief decides.

## 2. Where and when

**When.**
- **After DoD:** TCG opens on the camp after the Nah'ruk; the allies arrive the next day (WC3) [1].
- **The deadline:** at the parley, "When the Spears of Jade arrive … Less than three months from now" (GR7, quoting) [F]. Writers only (§8).
- **The crossing:** begins ch 10; "nine days from supply" when the water runs out (WT ch 20) [1]. **Its length in days: not found** [O]. Write "nights".
- **Season:** not found [O]. Write none. It is hot by day: the march is "conducted mostly at night" (W *Tavore Paran*) [1].
- **Hour of the last battles:** not found for either [O] (§10 Q4).

**The desert** (Ch 3, 5):
- **The Glass Desert** is in western Kolanse, east of Lether, with an "almost complete absence of water and life" (W *Glass Desert*) [1]. Glass underfoot: the soldiers raise crystal mounds over the dead (R17 S9) [1].
- **Icarias**, a dead city in the desert with the Unbound's gate (R17 S11–S15) [1]: a hard limit. "A dead city far off" at most.
- **The lake** appears under the army at the end (W *Glass Desert*, *Tavore Paran*; WT) [H].

**The southern road** (Ch 2, 4, 6):
- South of the desert toward Kolanse City (WC7; WT) [H]. Near the coast by ch 21: messengers come "from the coast" (WC21) [1]. No mountains, rivers or towns found [O].
- **Before Kolanse City:** the Kolansii line with trenches and a bank (R23.2) [1]; Kolanse Bay and the Spire beyond (WC23) [1].

**Kolanse:** drought; over-farmed thin soil, desertification, "teetering on the edge of starvation" (W *Kolanse*) [1]. Estobanse is its rich province, "thriving on springs" (W *Kolanse*) [1]; GR7 says it is named at the parley [F]. The North Citadel is in the north, under the Ses Mountains (W *North Citadel*) [1].

**The regulars' field** (Ch 7): west of the hill (WC24) [1]; a ridge Paran's relief comes over (R24.3 S29) [1]. Paran comes "from the east" (W *Tavore Paran*, p. 871) or from the north (WC24): **the sources disagree** [O]. Write "over the ridge".
