## Canon

1. **75_b4_ch5.js:327 (Orvas, Ramin), 75:327 and 75:481 (Haddat), 75:529 (Haddat)** — the Whirlwind Wall is written as old: Orvas "For three hundred years we have ridden round", Ramin "The Red Blades rode the edge of it once, in my father's day", Haddat's mother "said the Wall was a door" and "Here, she said, the Wall has teeth". In canon the Wall is the Whirlwind goddess's sandstorm, raised with the rising (supported from memory; hoc-canon §7.4 does not date it — David to check). **Fix:** make the old thing Raraku, not the Wall. Orvas: "The Khundryl do not go into the Holy Desert. We ride round. For three hundred years we have ridden round." Ramin: "The Red Blades rode the edge of the Holy Desert once, in my father's day." Haddat (327): "They said the Holy Desert had a door. You only had to know how to knock." (481): "My mother's people came in from the south. Never from here." (529): "A door," Haddat says. "My mother said the desert had a door." Severity: **canon**.
2. **76_b4_ch6.js:610** — Tangle "Black powder, the old kind, from the alchemists in Kan city": gunpowder does not exist in the Malazan world; munitions are Moranth alchemy. **Fix:** "Alchemist's paste, the old kind, from the shops in Kan city." Severity: **canon**.
3. **76_b4_ch6.js:662 and 77_b4_ch7.js:452** — Orvas quotes Gall's oath ("we are not Wickans, but we shall strive to be") twice; the brief allows it once at most. **Fix (77:452):** `"Gall says the Khundryl ride where the Wickans ride now," he says. He pulls the strap tight. "Tonight I would like to be a Wickan..."` Severity: **canon** (brief rule).
4. **72_b4_ch2.js:96 and 72:301** — "the trees along the Way where the High Fist's army was nailed up... You came in under them, the last mile, and saw" and Haddat "I counted the first hundred, coming in". Pormqual's army was crucified *after* the Chain was inside Aren (DG; Book Two Ch7 `b2_way_dead` shows it from the north wall, "over a day and a half"). **Fix (96):** `${b4Fresh() ?
