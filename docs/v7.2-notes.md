# v7.2 — the roster, and gear by role

David, Oct 26–27: "a full overhaul of the recruit system… a more uniform way to change out squad mates and gear needs to work more uniformly across books and squad mates." One system in all four books, in `src/38c_roster.js`.

## The roster rules

- **Six at most**: the sergeant and five, in every book (`ROSTER_CAP`). Everyone else the book has given the sergeant **waits at camp** (`S.camp`).
- **The core always walks** (unless the story takes someone out, as before): Book One and Three the Fourth (Brisk, Kettle, Tuft, Ohl); Book Two and Four Haddat, Sathi, Tangle, Marrow.
- **Bench-eligible** (can go to camp and come back):

| Book | Recruits and spares |
|---|---|
| One | Ellis, Vell |
| Two | Ramin (redblade), Keth (warlock), the Book One Old Hand (`S.f.b2_oldHand`) |
| Three | Sohra, Dannet (greysword), imported Ellis and Vell |
| Four | Ramin, Keth, Orvas; the Fourteenth's spares Tallow, Hessa, Tebbet, Darrut, Mirrel, Yorin, Rasp |

- **Book Four's five**: the five non-recruit places stay filled. A spare goes to the company only for another spare; a recruit walks on top of the five, and recruits swap freely within the six (`ROSTER_BOOKS.hoc.valid`). Rasp is not offered while Tangle walks (as before).
- **When changes are allowed**: Book One from Chapter 1; Book Two where its swaps always were (`b2SwapOpen`: Chapters 2–5, 6 before the silver, 7 before the wall); Book Three always; Book Four once Radde's muster is done. Locked, the screen still shows who walks and who waits, and says why.
- **A join with no room** puts the person at camp and says so plainly ("No room in the squad: Vell waits at camp. The roster can bring him in."). This is in `recruit()` itself, so every book's joining obeys it; `b3Join` and `b4Join` keep their own words.
- **Too many on load**: an old save with seven (Book One with Ellis and Vell, a Book Three import with both) sends the last recruit to camp, gear parked.
- **The story's own removals are unchanged** (deaths; Ellis through the rent; Ellis east to Toc; Book Two's Silanda, rearguard and partings; Ramin and Keth at the end of Book Two). Those people are not at camp and the roster never offers them.

## The screen

- **Where**: the choice **"The roster."** at every chapter fire (`ROSTER_FIRES`, added just before each fire's way out, with a tag: "2 at camp", or what the squad lacks), and a **Roster** tab beside Squad in the pack/journal/save modal, any time off the battlefield.
- **What it shows**: who walks (n of 6) and who waits, each with their role marks (healer, bow, munitions, sorcery, shield; "always walks" on the core), where the waiting wait, what the walking squad lacks, and on each waiting soldier what they would fill.
- **How**: tap "To camp" on a walker and/or "Bring in" on someone waiting; a line says what will happen ("Ellis to camp, Sohra walks.", or "No room: choose who goes to camp for Sohra.") with **Do it** and **Not now**. Either side can be chosen first; buttons that would break a rule are greyed. **Done** (or Close) goes back to the fire, shown again with what changed.
- **Back** drops a half-made choice first, then closes the screen (`rosterBackOne`, called from 90's `backTop`). The screen opens at the top and fits 390px.

## The per-book helpers, now through the roster

- `rosterIn(id)`, `rosterOut(id)`, `rosterSwap(out, in)`: each checks the book's rules and changes nothing if they say no. `rosterWaiting(id)`, `rosterCampList()`, `rosterCanIn/Out/Swap`, `rosterLacks()`, `rosterMarks(id)`, `rosterChoice(back)`, `openRoster(back)`.
- Book Two `b2SwapIn(id)`: room in the six, and they come with nobody going back; no room, the walking recruit goes back to their people. The map NPCs by each fire are kept.
- Book Three `b3Join`, `b3SwapIn`: as before, through the roster. New **`b3CanSend(id)`**: she waits *and* there is room or another recruit to send back. The fires' "Send for Sohra/Dannet" choices in Chapters 5, 6 and 7 now use it, so a squad full of the Fourth (the full import) is never offered a send-for it cannot keep, and no fire says she came when she didn't (the bug at 65:899, 66:508/1152, 67:1229/1236). The roster does that case.
- Book Four `b4Join`, `b4SwapIn` (room: in; full: the first walking recruit goes back), `b4SwapNew` (spare for spare), `b4Offers()` (only those who can come now).
- `unrecruit()` now takes the gear off (it used to leave it on: an item could be worn twice). `letGo()` and `unrecruit()` park the gear; `kill()` clears it and the camp entry.

## Gear by role

Every soldier has `TPL[id].roles` (the kinds of gear they can use); every item has a `role` (or a signature `who` list). `canWear(id, item)` = the signature list if there is one, else role match; `any` fits everyone. The sheet, `placeGear`, `allocateGear`, `gearValue`, `equip` and the item viewer all use it. The tables are `TPL_ROLES` and `ITEM_ROLE` in 38c; an item not listed is `any` (or keeps a writer's `who` list as a signature piece). `B4_WEARS_LIKE`/`b4WhoPatch` are gone.

| Role | Gear | Who has it |
|---|---|---|
| heavy | heavy armour (officer's scale, the otataral glove) | sergeant, Brisk, Haddat, Ramin, Dannet, Hessa, Darrut |
| mail | mail shirts (auxiliary mail, Aren Guard mail) | the heavy seven, plus Kettle, Tangle, Rasp, Orvas |
| shield | shields | sergeant, Brisk, Haddat, Ramin, Dannet, Hessa, Darrut |
| light | cloaks, hides, leathers, rope-and-hook | everyone but Brisk, Haddat, Ramin, Dannet, Hessa, Darrut |
| blade | knives, short blades, sabres, the flint, the Dogslayer's sword | everyone but Tuft |
| heavyweapon | axes, maces, tulwar, longsword, the K'ell blade | the heavy seven |
| reach | spears, lances, the halberd | the heavy seven, plus Sathi, Sohra, Orvas |
| ranged | bows and crossbows | sergeant, Kettle, Ellis, Sathi, Tangle, Sohra, Tallow, Tebbet, Rasp |
| satchel | sapper's kit (the short spade) | Kettle, Tangle, Rasp |
| healkit | (none yet) | Ohl, Marrow, Mirrel |
| focus | charms and foci (cadre token, Andii-grey cloak) | Tuft, Ohl, Marrow, Keth, Sohra, Hessa, Mirrel, Yorin |
| any | trinkets and the rest | everyone |

Fixed exclusions: Darrut and the Aren Guard mail; Haddat and "Coltaine's finger" (now any); Book Three's bow, shield, spear and blades for Sohra, Dannet, Ellis and Vell; `gearValue`'s front line is now whoever takes a shield or mail (adds Dannet, Orvas, Rasp, the sappers). Lost by role (deliberately): Kettle the Beklite's spear, Vell the Seerdomin's mace.

**Signature pieces kept personal** (`GEAR_SIG`): the Second Army badge (Brisk and the sergeant: Tav's regiment), the Moranth chit (Kettle), the water-sellers' seal (Haddat).

**Who goes to camp leaves their gear in the pack** (`S.parked[id]` remembers it, unworn and usable by others); coming back they get back what they wore if it is still free. `gearTidy()` (on every map entry and fight start, on load, and on every roster move): one wearer per item; nobody off the squad wears anything; a squadmate back from wherever the story sent them gets theirs again.

## Transfers carry everyone

- **Book One → Three**: Ellis and Vell come with their gear (worn, or parked if they waited at camp), loyalty and picks; anyone waiting at camp at the end counts as walking north. Seven arrivals: the last waits at camp.
- **Book Two → Four**: Ramin and Keth, if they went to the cells or the Wickan children alive, arrive with their Book Two gear (parked for them until they join: `S.parked`), loyalty and picks; their items come in the kit.
- **Book One → Two**: the Old Hand gets their Book One gear (into the kit, parked, worn on joining), loyalty and picks (`dgImport` now carries `gear` and `picks`; `b2OldHand` applies them).

## Migrations (old saves)

`migrate()` (38c wrapper): `S.camp` and `S.parked` are created; Book Two/Three/Four's `S.f.b2_allyGear` / `b3_allyGear` / `b4_allyGear` become `S.parked` and the flags go; recruits waiting by the old flags (Book Three/Four flag 2, Book Two allies off with their people) are put in `S.camp`; a squad over six sends its last recruits to camp; `gearTidy()` fixes any item worn twice and parks gear worn by anyone off the squad.
