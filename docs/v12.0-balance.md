# v12.0 balance: every fight in Book Nine (*Dust on the Boots*), tuned per fight

**David's targets** (Fri Nov 6, 2026; for Book Nine Tue Nov 10, 2026: "tune Book Nine to the series targets, Book Nine tables only"), measured as the smart bot's first-try win %, per fight:
- **Soldier** about 90% (accept 85–97%). Kept near 100%: the first fight of each squad's book (`b9_c1_looters`, the Third; `b9_c2_drill`, the Letherii) and the survive fights (`b9_c2_drill` survive 4, `b9_c4_herd` survive 4, `b9_c6_train` survive 5, `b9_c6_dusk` survive 4, `b9_c7_dark` survive 5).
- **Bridgeburner** about 75–80% (accept 65–85%), and no fight under 60%.
- **The mortal fight** `b9_c7_rearguard` should be able to cost lives; deaths reported per fight.
- Soldier never harder than Bridgeburner on the same fight, beyond the run-to-run noise.

**Where the numbers live** (src/160_book9.js only; no other book's table, no engine file was touched):
- `B9SOLDIER` — Soldier only, merged into `SOLDIER_FIGHT` by fight id. 16 rows; the two first fights and the survive fights have none.
- `B9GAP` — Bridgeburner only, 22 rows, one a fight (`{ac +, atk +, hp ×, dmg +}`, as `B8GAP`), applied in Book Nine's own `mkFoe` wrapper.
- `B9GAP_ROW` — `{ac:5, atk:6, hp:3.75, dmg:8}`, for a `b9_` fight added later without its own row (it was `null`).

**The harness's Book Nine** (`tools/balance.mjs --book=dod`, `setupDOD`): both squads in one save; the Third at 19/20/21/22 in Chapters 1/3/5/7, the Letherii at 11/12/13 in Chapters 2/4/6; recruits by then; Rations at 5 ('fed', the brief's middle road, so no hunger cut); a Deck card drawn at random from the chapter's pool (one source of the noise).

**How it was tuned**: as Book Eight (docs/v11.0-balance.md). Smart bot, `--noellis=0 --naive=0 --par=2`, one balance job at a time (this machine has 2 CPUs). The untuned book won every fight 100% on both difficulties with 0–13% health lost. Book Eight's coarse ladder, level q: armour +⌊q/2⌋, to hit +⌈q/2⌉, health ×(1 + q/4), damage +⌈q/2⌉ + 2, capped at q 40 here (Book Eight: 30) because the Third at 19–22 shrugged off q 30 in three fights. Two calibration probes on every fight (q 10 and q 14, 6 runs each) seeded each fight's level on each side; the tuner (scratchpad `tune9/auto9.py`, adapted from `tune8/auto8.py`: sequential jobs, bigger steps for a survive fight won 100%) bisected each fight at 10 runs, confirmed at 20 (pooled at the same level), then three fix passes. The final tables were built into src and measured unpatched at 20 runs on both difficulties; the fights still outside their band got hand rows between ladder levels and were measured again at 20 runs (Soldier `b9_c1_stores`, `b9_c2_bout`, `b9_c2_wagons`, `b9_c3_road`, `b9_c4_forage`, `b9_c6_line`, `b9_c7_rearguard`; Bridgeburner `b9_c1_stores`, `b9_c1_brawl`, `b9_c2_bout`, `b9_c6_line`). Twenty runs swing about ±10, so a fight at 85% here may be 75–95%.

The probes showed a split book: the long two-stage fights against the short-tails (`b9_c4_raid`, `b9_c6_line`, `b9_c7_rearguard`) fall off a cliff at a low level (q 4–7), while the Third's fights against dogs, deserters and one bull barely move until q 20–40, since the Third at 19–22 kills them before they act.

**The mortal fight** (`b9_c7_rearguard`: three short-tail soldiers and a sorcerer, then a brute and two more at dusk, with two Bonehunter heavies beside the Third; whoever falls stays down).
- **Bridgeburner** `{ac:0, atk:1, hp:1.25, dmg:3}`: 18 of 20 won (90%; the tuner's 20 at the same row: 80%), 1.0 dead a fight counting the two losses (a loss kills everyone down, the sergeant aside); in the 18 wins, 5 cost at least one squadmate (10 dead in all, up to 4 in one fight). It sits on a cliff: one ladder step harder (q 2) won 40%, q 4 won 20% with 4 dead a fight, so the easiest step above none was kept.
- **Soldier** `{ac:2, atk:2, hp:1.8, dmg:4}`: 18 of 20 won (90%), 0.6 dead a fight; 2 of the 18 wins cost one squadmate. The tuner's level (q 4, health ×2.0, 85%) put Soldier's foe 1.13× Bridgeburner's by tools/fightrow_test.mjs's threat measure, so its health came down to ×1.8.
- **At the Rations floor** (David, Nov 10: the column's pot tops the Third up to 2 before the rearguard, so the worst the Third can go in is 'hungry', every squadmate −10% health), measured with a patch setting Rations to 2 at the fight's start: Soldier 80% won (16 of 20), 1.15 dead a fight, 3 of 16 wins cost a life; Bridgeburner 65% won (13 of 20), 2.4 dead a fight, and every one of the 13 wins cost a life (13 dead in wins, up to 4). A hungry Third on Bridgeburner should expect to bury someone; a fed Third (5+) usually walks off whole.

## Outside the band, and why

- **Soldier near 100%** (above 97%) besides the seven kept there by rule: `b9_c2_bout` (100%; one step harder, q 12 → measured 80% in the tuner, the same row measured 100% here: a cliff, pooled 90% over 40), `b9_c4_raid` (100%; held down by the Soldier-under-Bridgeburner cap, since Bridgeburner's row is already at 60–85% and a Soldier row one step harder came out 1.28× Bridgeburner's foe), `b9_c7_trail` (100%; the same row won 95% in the tuner, pooled 97.5%: three Wastes dogs at q 28 against the Third at 22, optional).
- **Soldier under 85%**: `b9_c3_road` 80% — a cliff: a hand row a hair easier (`{ac:6, atk:6, hp:4.1, dmg:9}`) won 100%, q 13 won 75%; kept on the harder side, inside the ±10 swing of 90.
- **Bridgeburner over 85%**: the survive fights `b9_c2_drill`, `b9_c6_train`, `b9_c6_dusk`, `b9_c7_dark` (100%; `b9_c4_herd` came down to 80%). The bot holds a survive fight well, as Book Eight's `b8_c4_edge`; the rows were pushed up to q 35–40 and the squad loses 47–82% of its health holding them, but it holds. `b9_c6_dusk` (survive 4) is at the ladder's cap and still loses only 7%: it is one short-tail soldier at the top of the field and a sorcerer from round 2, against six and two escorts stepping back, and four rounds are over before they do much; only a change to the fight itself (closer starts or a longer hold) would bite, and that is a chapter change, not a table one. `b9_c5_bull` 90% (one maddened bhederin: the Third pins it, and a Bridgeburner row one step harder won 65%; the tuner's 20 at this row won 85%); `b9_c7_rearguard` 90% (above; 85% pooled over 40, and 65% at the Rations floor); `b9_c7_trail` 95% (75% in the tuner at the same row, pooled 85%).
- **Bridgeburner under 65%**: `b9_c4_raid` 60% (the tuner's 20 at the same row won 85%: 72.5% pooled over 40). One hand step easier (`{ac:2, atk:2, hp:1.9, dmg:4}`) won 95%, a cliff, so the row was left where the pool sits in the band. No fight measured under 60%.
- **The bull's Soldier row is very high** (`{ac:20, atk:20, hp:11.0, dmg:22}`, near the cap): the bot stuns and pins a single beast so well that it barely lands a blow (14% health lost, one stalemate in 20). A player who does not pin it will find it far harder than the bot does; the fight is optional (+2 Rations if killed). The same holds, less sharply, for the dogs (`b9_c3_dogs`, `b9_c5_dogs`, `b9_c7_trail`) and the Third's first fights.
- **Soldier never harder than Bridgeburner**: in every fight the measured Soldier win is at or above Bridgeburner's (the rearguard ties at 90/90), and every Soldier foe is at most 1.1× Bridgeburner's by tools/fightrow_test.mjs's threat measure (Bridgeburner's blow ×1.2).
