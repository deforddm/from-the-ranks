## What the novel gives us

- **Where and when.** Darujhistan in summer, some years after *Memories of Ice*, from the New Year on (R1, W) [H]. Black Coral, Rake's city on the site of Coral, where Itkovian's barrow has become the cult of the Redeemer (W, R4) [H]. The Lamatath plain, and the realm inside Dragnipur (W) [1].
- **Darujhistan.**
  - The Assassins' Guild has a contract on **retired Malazans**. Its first killers die on Picker and the K'rul's Bar Bridgeburners (R1) [1].
  - Murillio is stabbed (R1) and later killed in a duel by Gorlas Vidikas (§(e), W) [H].
  - Gorlas Vidikas, Shardan Lim and Hanut Orr are on the Council (R1) [1].
  - Cutter comes home. Barathol and Spite arrive (W) [1].
  - Mallet dies defending the bar (§(e), W) [1].
- **Black Coral** (R4, R22) [1]:
  - Spinnock Durav and Endest Silann serve Rake.
  - A former Seerdomin guards the Redeemer's pilgrims.
  - The Dying God's cult works on the pilgrim camp through Gradithan and Salind.
  - Spindle and Monkrat get the camp's children out.
- **The road** (R4, R22, W) [1]:
  - Nimander's Andii go to Bastion with Clip, where the Dying God's body lies, and Kallor walks the plain.
  - Karsa and Samar Dev come to Darujhistan with Traveller.
  - Spinnock holds a crossroads against Kallor (R22).
- **The end** (R22, §(e), §(f)) [H]:
  - The Hounds break Darujhistan's wall, and the moon is seen to have shattered.
  - Hood comes into Dragnipur with the armies of the dead, by a bargain with Rake, and Iskar Jarak (Whiskeyjack) leads them.
  - Rake dies in Darujhistan, by his own choice, at Traveller's hand.
  - Mother Dark comes back to Black Coral.
  - After Book Eight, Hood can't answer as a patron. The Guardians of the Gate can (§(f)).

## Why the Fourth fits

- **The contract is for retired Malazans** (R1). On Book Three's `daru` road, the Fourth already lives in Darujhistan, a few years out of the Host. So the Guild's list has their names on it too. That is Chapter 1's hook, and it needs no canon to bend.
- **Black Coral is built on Coral.** The Fourth fought in Coral's streets in Book Three and saw the Shield Anvil's barrow raised. Its Redeemer is the man they saw go under the gifts.
- **Whiskeyjack** leads the dead at the end, and the Fourth served under him in Book One.
