## 1. The campaign, in order (game Chapters 1–4)

### 1.1 Game Chapter 1, The Drownings: Letheras (novel ch 2, 4, 6)

- **No Letherii unit is on stage in ch 1–5.** The only soldiers are the Royal Guard and court officers in Letheras, and the seal poachers' crews in Edur waters. [H] W1–W5, R1–R5
- **Letheras places** in these chapters. [H] W2, W4, R2, R4, W *Letheras*, except where marked
  - The Old Palace, built round three Jaghut towers and the Azath; its throne room, with an alcove used for eavesdropping. The Cedance lies deep beneath it.
  - The Eternal Domicile, the new palace: seventeen years of work, unfinished, suffering clay heave.
  - The Azath tower behind the palace, with a yard of prisoners.
  - The **Quillas Canal**, about 100 paces wide, in the Drownings District, watched from the Low Walk and the tiers above. [H] W4, W *The Drownings*
  - Pursers' District [1] (W *Letheras*, ch 4). Huldo's and Rild's (restaurants) [1] W2, W4. Errant's Heel [H] W6, R6. Tehol's house with its roof; Shand's workshop.
- **The Drownings.** [1] W *The Drownings*, except where marked
  - The accused is weighted with the fine in coins, tied to a rope so the coins can be recovered, and made to swim the canal. Reach the far side alive and you go free; most drown [H] W *The Drownings*, W4, R4. Extra weight can be added if the swimmer looks capable.
  - Corpses are dumped back into the river: "Guilty as sludge".
  - Who is sentenced: offenders who cannot pay to expunge their records (W *Letheras*); "criminals" (W4); "Indebted Letherii" (R4). **The game reads it as a debtor's court:** a fine you cannot pay goes into the canal with you. [H] W *Letheras*, R4
  - Fines in docks, men / women: theft, kidnapping, failure to repay a debt, damage to property and murder 500 / 250; embezzlement, assault without cause, and cursing the Empty Throne, the king or the queen 300 / 150; loitering, voiding in public and disrespect 100 / 50.
  - Wagers are laid on Drownings, Strokes, Risings, Flailings, Flounderings and Vanishings.
  - The Errant's festival brings **Special Drownings** for the most notorious criminals, with frenzied wagering [H] W *The Drownings*, W *Letheras* (ch 6).
  - Named swimmers: Ublala Pung (4,300 docks; walks the bed and goes free, ch 4) and Shurq Elalle (200 docks; drowned long before, kept undead by a curse). [H] W4, R4
- **Court and army in ch 2 and 4.** [H] W2, W4, R2, R4, except where marked
  - Preda **Unnutal Hebaz** commands the army. Brys Beddict is Finadd and the new King's Champion. Moroch Nevath is Finadd and Prince Quillas's bodyguard ("head of the Prince's Guard", R2). Gerun Eberict is a Finadd of the Royal Guard.
  - The throne-room quarrel over Eberict's place in the delegation; Moroch starts to draw, Brys draws, Quillas storms out. Both the Preda and Turudal Brizad are in that scene (ch 2, HC p. 69) **[T]**.
  - Eberict holds the **King's Leave**, immunity from conviction, for saving the King four years ago; he has since killed 31 citizens and is "the most feared man in Letheras". [H] W4, R4
  - Brys joined the King's Guard to escape debt [1] R4. **For the game: the army is one road out of debt.**
- **The poachers' ghost ships (ch 3, offstage in Lether).** Hannan Mosag destroys the Letherii seal poachers in Calach Bay; shadow wraiths sail the emptied ships back to Lether with the dead crews aboard [H] W3, R3. Where and when they make landfall is never given [O]. A Beneda warrior washed ashore was "killed by Letherii sorcery", so mages sailed with the poachers [H] W3, R3; who they were is open.
- **When.** No dates in ch 2–4; the Great Meeting with the Edur is coming, "in two cycles of the moon" at ch 1 [1] W1. The delegation has not sailed by ch 6 [1] R6.
- **Deaths:** one spy (Kettle kills him, ch 4); nothing else on the page in Letheras. [H] W4, R4

### 1.2 Game Chapter 2, Trate: the harbour after the wave (novel ch 6; ch 15 for Trate still standing)

- **Trate.** Lether's northernmost city, a walled port with trebuchets and "ballistae armed with hull-breaching quarrels", a lighthouse over the harbour front, and the North Gate [1] W *Trate*. Market stalls sell Fent and Nerek curios and K'Chain Che'Malle bones and teeth nobody recognises; the city is known for its flower dancers [1] W *Trate*. Letheras is "leagues to the southeast" [1] W6.
  - **Tarancede Tower**: seven storeys of basalt on an artificial island of rocks in the harbour's south, no doors or windows, its top ringed with obsidian tiles linked to the Ceda's chamber; one of ten border towers [1] W *Tarancede Tower*. The Ceda watches the bay through a tile matching it [H] W *Tarancede Tower*, R6.
  - Garrison: the **Trate Legion**. Its resident sorceress is **Nekal Bara**. [1] W *Trate*, W *Letherii Army*, W *Kraig's Landing*
- **The wave (ch 6).** Three ships approach. The pilot scow goes out, then backs away as "shadowy shapes swarm through the rigging"; an alarm bell sounds. A bound sea spirit carries the ships on its back on a huge wave into the harbour; it "crashes down on ships and people alike, leaving devastation in its wake", then withdraws. [H] W6, R6
  - The ships are "harvest ships", the poachers sent back as Mosag's answer; aboard are corpses and wraiths [H] R6, W *Trate*. The surge carries water, fish and crustaceans into the crowded docks [1] W *Trate*.
  - **No death toll, no damage list and no soldiers' response** are given [O] W6, R6. Season and time of day: [O].
  - The spirit is a forgotten, hungry spirit once worshipped in Trate [H] W16, R16. It comes back in ch 16.
- **Trate still stands in ch 15.** Seren comes downriver by barge to Trate at night and finds Buruk hanged in his house, on a street just in from the riverside warehouses [H] W15, R15, W *Buruk the Pale*. Trate is Letherii-held until ch 16 (§7).
- **The squad's place [C].** No Whitefinder is recorded at Trate. The battalion's station is High Fort (§2), a march from Trate. **Claude's call:** the squad's company is sent to Trate on loan after the wave, to help the Trate Legion clear the harbour and stand watch on the waterfront, and stays on that duty into ch 15. This is why it is not with the battalion when the battalion is training in the south at the war's start (§2).
- **Deaths:** uncounted ("devastation") [O]. A Nerek grandmother in Letheras is killed by Eberict's guards [H] W6, R6.

## 2. The army the squad serves in

- **Size and doctrine.** Up to 600,000 soldiers from Lether and its protectorates [1] W *Letherii Army*. Doctrine in three phases: a rolling mobile defence to draw the enemy in, a counter-stroke at his supply lines, then encirclement, with the navies raiding his homeland [1]. Hidden caches of food, water and stores throughout the kingdom [1]. The army leans on sorcery more than on fighting hand to hand [1]. Legions carry attached "masters of sorcery" trained by Ceda Kuru Qan, whose mages link units instantly [1]. Purpose-built military roads; legions are rivals [1].
- **Ranks.** [H] DP, W2, except where marked
  - **Preda**: the army's commander (DP: "Preda (Commander) of the Letherii army"). The TBH glossary calls it "Captain"; the MT and RG glossaries say general [1] W *Preda*. Unnutal Hebaz holds it.
  - **Atri-Preda**: a field or garrison command below Preda (Yan Tovis at Fent Reach; Bivatt in RG) [1] DP, W *Letherii Army*. The exact relation is [M].
  - **Finadd**: captain (Brys, Moroch, Eberict, Tullid).
  - **Ceda**: the King's Sorcerer.
  - **No rank below Finadd is named** in any source [O]. **[C]:** the game says "sergeant" for the squad's leader, as a plain English word, and never invents a Letherii title for it. The company's officer is a Finadd.
- **Kit.** **Crossbows are unknown in Lether** [1] W *Letherii Army*. **[C]:** the Indebted conscript carries a bow, not a crossbow, so the plan's "Indebted crossbow" becomes **the Indebted bowman** (§16). The Dresh ballistae are engines, not hand weapons [1] W *Battle*.
- **Horse.** Lether had no horses until it conquered **Bluerose**. Its cavalry is badly organised despite dozens of Bluerose officer-trainers, and Bluerose supplied poor saddles and stirrups on purpose; Bluerose units are posted far off over doubts of their loyalty [1] W *Letherii Army*, [H] with W19, R19 for the sabotage. Bluerose's Onyx Wizards are outlawed [1] DP.
- **The dead's debts.** A death pension goes to the families of the fallen, **but heirs are liable for lost equipment and the dead soldier's debts** [1] W *Letherii Army*. This is the book's Debt system's hardest edge.
- **Other forces named in MT.** [1] W *Letherii Army*, except where marked
  - Trate: Trate Legion; Cold Clay Battalion.
  - The Reach: Fent Reach's garrison (surrendered); First Maiden Fort (evacuated).
  - High Fort: the fort's own rotating garrison, the Grass Jackets Brigade and the Whitefinder Battalion as its station [1] W *High Fort*; the Queen's Battalion and Quillas Brigade at the battle.
  - Fort Shake: the **Harridict Brigade**, which loses it to Tomad Sengar's army (where and when: [O]).
  - The Manse: the **Artisan Battalion**, which abandons it.
  - Old Katter and Awl: the **Riven Brigade** and the **Snakebelt Battalion** withdraw to defend Letheras.
  - Brans Keep: Crimson Rampant Brigade, Snakebelt Battalion, Riven Brigade, King's Battalion, Merchants' Battalion, Artisan Battalion, Whitefinder Battalion [H] W *Letherii Army*, W *Battle of Brans Keep*.
  - Letheras: the City Garrison (stations that ring bells to call other units, ch 20), the Royal Guard, Palace Guards, the Prince's Guard (Moroch), the Royal Engineers (guild-hire only) [1] W *Letheras*, DP, R2. Nifadas's eunuch bodyguards, the Rulith [1] DP.
  - Brous: a small garrison under Finadd Arlidas Tullid [H] R21, W *Brous*.
  - Not found: a "Harbour" unit, a "Prince's Battalion", any Bluerose unit in MT (the Bluerose Battalion is RG's) [O].
- **The Whitefinder Battalion: everything found.**
  - Its station is **High Fort**, with the Grass Jackets and the fort's rotating garrison [1] W *High Fort*.
  - **When the war began it was away training near First Reach**, five days' march from High Fort by good road, and the Preda ordered it back north (ch 14) [1] W *High Fort*. First Reach is upriver of Brous on the Brous River [1] W *Brous*.
  - At High Fort (ch 18): not named [O].

R23 names no unit [O].
  - Not in RG's Eastern Army list [1] W *Letherii Army*.

- **The army and debt.** Brys joined the King's Guard to escape debt; Hull exiled himself to escape "gold's trap" [1] R4. **The army is a way out of debt for some, and the game's squad are four of them.**

## 3. Lether: debt, the Holds, the peoples

- **Debt.** Debt "bound the entire kingdom, defining every relationship" [1] R1, quoting the text. The **Indebted** are a class: Udinaas is Indebted and so cannot hope to marry Feather Witch [1] R1; the Nerek hauling Buruk's wagons are Indebted [H] W2, R2. Debts pass to heirs (the soldier's rule, §2) [1]. Debt-collectors in ch 1–5: [O]. The Rat Catchers' Guild is background only, never met [1] DP.
