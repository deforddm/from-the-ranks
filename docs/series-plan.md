# From the Ranks: the series plan (squads return where possible)

Thu Nov 5, 2026, 9:45 AM PST (the Book Six row: shipped as v9.0.0; the Book Seven row: the Fourth's road corrected, as the Book Six plan said). Before that Thu Oct 29, 2026, 5:58 PM PDT (the Book Five row). First written Tue Oct 13, 2026, 8:45 PM PDT. David's direction: no skipping ahead (one book per novel, in order), each book playable on its own, and the squads come back wherever the novel gives them a place. A returning squad carries its level, gear, picks and tricks across on a transfer (Book Three set the pattern in v6.1.0). Canon placements below are from the novels as I know them; each book's canon sheet re-checks them before any writing.

| Book | Novel | Where it happens | Who can return |
|---|---|---|---|
| One | Gardens of the Moon | Pale → Darujhistan | the Fourth (original) |
| Two | Deadhouse Gates | Hissar → Aren | Book Two's squad (original) |
| Three | Memories of Ice | Pale → Coral | the Fourth |
| Four | House of Chains | Seven Cities: Aren → Raraku with the Adjunct's Fourteenth Army | **Book Two's squad**: Aren's survivors of the Seventh are folded into the Fourteenth. The Fourth stay on Genabackis (no place). |
| Five | Midnight Tides | Lether, before the Malazans arrive: Letheras → Trate, Kraig's Landing, High Fort → Brans Keep → the fall of Letheras | **Settled (shipped v8.0.0, Oct 29, 2026):** a new Letherii cast, the Sixth Company of the Whitefinder Battalion (*Indebted*); neither earlier squad returns. Its `emperor` road (alive in Letheras under the Edur) is the one Book Seven may import. |
| Six | The Bonehunters | Seven Cities: the Lato Odhan, Y'Ghatan, the north coast and G'danisban, the Kokakal shore, the sea, Malaz City | **Settled (shipped v9.0.0, Nov 5, 2026):** *Bones in the Walls*, **both squads, taking turns** in one save: the Third of Blistig's 9th with the Fourteenth (Ch 1, 3, 5, 7; transfers a Book Four save on `line`) and the Fourth with Onearm's Host (Ch 2, 4, 6; transfers a Book Three save on `host`). Levels 12 to 18. The Third's roads out: `adjunct` (with the fleet: the road Book Seven imports), `jetty` (left in Malaz City), `wickans` (gone with the Wickans). The Fourth stays with the Host in Seven Cities. |
| Seven | Reaper's Gale | Lether, the Fourteenth's invasion | Book Two's squad on Book Six's `adjunct` road, with the Fourteenth; **Book Five's Letherii on the `emperor` road**. The Host doesn't go to Lether, so the Fourth's next book is Eight. **Kettle keeps her name** (David, Oct 13, 2026): when the Fourth meet or hear of the Azath child Kettle, our Kettle remarks that the girl's parents had good taste, or something in that vein. |
| Eight | Toll the Hounds | Darujhistan and Black Coral | **The Fourth** on Book Three's "daru" or "stand down" roads, a few years on (and, if canon gives the Host a place, Book Six's Fourth). |
| Nine–Ten | Dust of Dreams, The Crippled God | the Wastelands, Kolanse | Whoever is still with the Bonehunters. |

Rules carried forward: the transfer offers any finished save of the right road; levels keep growing per book (the cap will rise again with each returning book: 18 from Book Six); Book One and Two saves keep their own caps.
