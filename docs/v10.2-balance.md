# v10.2 balance: every fight in Books One to Seven, tuned per fight

**David's targets** (approved Fri Nov 6, 2026, 10:49 PM PST), measured as the smart bot's first-try win %, per fight:
- **Soldier** about 90% (accept 85–97%). Kept near 100%: the first fight of each book, and every "survive N rounds" fight. Book Five's last barricade (Ch7) comes back to about 90% (it was eased to always-win: B5SOFT is now empty).
- **Bridgeburner** about 75–80% (accept 65–85%), and no fight under 60%.

**Engine change**: the ward cap goes from 25% to 15% of max health (`wardCap`, 31c; tools/ward_test.mjs; spec §2 and the notes). Every "final" number below is on the 15% cap.

**Where the numbers live** (31c unless named; nothing changes squad stats, abilities, xp or story):
- `SOLDIER_FIGHT` — Soldier only, every book, keyed by fight id (`'id:2'` for a second area, else the id covers both). A fight's own row *replaces* its chapter's `SOLDIER_LATE` row (so a row from Ch3 on already includes the chapter's extra). Book Four's `B4GAP` `s` column still applies under it. `SOLDIER_SKIP` (Book Three's Coral mortal fights) is honoured: they have no row and keep their weight (David: as they are).
- `B1GAP`, `B2GAP`, `B3GAP_FIGHT`, `B4GAP_FIGHT` — Bridgeburner only, Books One to Four, *on top of* everything else (Books Three and Four keep their chapter rows `B3GAP`/`B4GAP` on both difficulties).
- Books Five to Seven: their existing Bridgeburner rows were moved in place (`B5GAP_FIGHT` in 080, `B6GAP` in 100, `B7GAP` in 120); a fight that had no row got one.
- `mkFoe` (31) applies `soldierLate()` (Soldier) or `bbFight()` (Bridgeburner): `{hp ×, atk +, dmg +, ac +}`.

**How it was tuned**: tools/balance.mjs, smart bot, `--noellis=0 --naive=0`, with each candidate table injected by `--patch` (no rebuild). An automatic tuner (scratchpad `tune/auto.py`) moved each fight along one ladder, health first and damage every fourth step (level L > 0: hp ×(1 + 0.05L), dmg + L/4, to hit + L/10; L < 0: hp ×(1 − 0.04|L|), dmg − (|L|+2)/4, to hit − |L|/6), bisecting between a level measured too easy and one too hard, at 10 runs a step, then confirmed every fight at 20 runs (pooled with the last 10-run pass at the same level); a fight outside the band at 20 runs went back to the tuner. Armour was never moved (a point of armour swings a d20 fight too far). Ten runs swing about ±15, twenty about ±10, so a fight at 85% here may be 75–95%.

**Saturated fights**: a "survive N rounds" fight the bot never loses on Bridgeburner even at hp ×1.8 and +5 damage (Book Four's two brawls) was put back to level 8 (hp ×1.4, +2 damage) and left near 100%: the bot holds four rounds of fists whatever they hit for. Listed under the book.


**Progress** (tuning order Four, Three, One, Two, Five, Six, Seven): Book Four done, Book Three to do, Book One to do, Book Two to do, Book Five to do, Book Six to do, Book Seven to do. A fresh run resumes from the first "to do": the tables already in src are the finished books; scratchpad tune/<book>.state.json holds a book in progress.
