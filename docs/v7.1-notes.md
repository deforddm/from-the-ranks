## The pool and the muster

- **`HOC_NEW = ['tallow', 'hessa', 'tebbet', 'darrut', 'mirrel', 'yorin', 'rasp']`.** The chapters' `b41New()`…`b47New()` read it, so the three count as new soldiers everywhere.
- **`newStateHOC`** still puts a provisional fill in the old order (`HOC_NEW.slice(0, 4 − mates)`), so any tool or save that skips the muster has a squad of five. It sets `S.f.b4_musterDue` (the places owed: 1 on a fresh start, 0 to 4 on a transfer) and `S.f.b4_fill` (the provisional ids). `S.picksDue` is counted without the fill: the fill's talent comes when the muster takes them on.
- **Chapter 1's start.** While `b4MusterDue()` (Book Four, places owed, not yet mustered), the intro says "The Fourteenth owes the squad N soldier(s)…", and its "Into the line" goes to **`b4_muster`** instead of `b4_c1_start`.
- **`b4_muster`** (Sergeant Radde with the company roll). The first time it opens (`b4MusterOpen`), the provisional fill goes back (out of the squad, its gear, loyalty and picks cleared). It lists every spare with a line about them. Each choice: **"Take <Name> (<role>)."** with a tag when the squad lacks what they fill. A pick (`b4Take`) recruits them (70's `recruit` gives the veteran picks for the levels passed; the talent pick waits) and the node reopens with the newcomer's arrival line and the places left. When the places are full the only choice is **"Into the line."** (`b4MusterDone`: `S.f.b4_mustered = 1`, `S.picksDue` recounted, so the newcomers' talents are picked next), and it goes to `b4_c1_start`. No "Not now": the choice is made before the march.
- **The spares** (`b4SpareList()`): the pool, less anyone in the squad or dead, and **Rasp only while Tangle is not in the squad**.
- **The marks** (`b4Lacks()`, `b4SpareMark(id)`), counted after every pick:

| Lacks | When | Filled by | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| healer | nobody with Mend (Ohl, Marrow, Mirrel) | Mirrel | "no healer in the squad" |
| bow | nobody but the sergeant with range 4 or more who is not a sapper (Sathi, Tebbet, Tallow's sling) | Tebbet, Tallow | "no bow in the squad" |
| munitions | nobody with sharpers (Tangle, Rasp) | Rasp | "no sapper in the squad" |
| sorcery | no caster who is not a healer (`magic` without Mend), and nobody with spirit-craft (Keth) | Yorin | "no sorcery in the squad" |

  Hessa and Darrut fill none of the four (Hessa's prayers are not counted as the squad's sorcery). Tested: the sergeant, Haddat and Ramin lack all four; a fresh start (Haddat, Tangle, Marrow) lacks only a bow and sorcery, and is not offered Rasp.
- **`b4Places()`**: 5 less the living squad (the sergeant counts).

## The fire offer, for a save from before the muster

- Every chapter's fire has **`b4SpareChoice(back)`**: "Radde has spares from the company." (tag "swap a new soldier"). It shows while **`b4SparesOpen()`**: Book Four, `S.f.b4_musterDue` was never set (a save begun under v7.0), not yet mustered, at least one of the pool in the squad, and a spare to offer. A v7.1 game never sees it.
- **`b4_spares`**: Radde, the spares, the same "Take <Name> (<role>)." choices and marks, and "Keep the squad as it is." (`S.f.b4_mustered = 'kept'`, back to the fire).
- **`b4_spares_out`**: "And who goes back to the company?" One choice per new soldier in the squad ("<Name> goes back to the company."), or "Not that one after all."
- **`b4SwapNew(out, in)`**: one for one, the newcomer in the leaver's place in the order; the leaver's gear goes back to the pack (`S.gear[out] = {}`, the kit is kept); the newcomer's veteran picks come with `recruit`, and the talent pick is due. `S.f.b4_spareSwaps` lists `[out, in]` pairs.
- **`b4_spares_done`**: the leaver goes, the newcomer's arrival line; "Another." (while there is a spare and a new soldier to swap) or "That will do. Back to the fire." (`S.f.b4_mustered = 'fire'`). After that the offer is gone for the book.
- `S.f.b4_spareBack` holds the fire node to return to.

## The new abilities (every number)

- **Sky-Fire** (`b4_bolt`, Yorin; Serc, strain 2): an enemy within **5** in sight (**6** with Storm Eye). An attack roll at Yorin's to-hit **+2** (+3 with Storm Eye): **2d6 + 2 + the level's damage** on a hit. Sputtered in the Dust: **1d6 + half that**.
- **The Sky's Shove** (`b4_gust`, Yorin; Serc, strain 3): an enemy within **4** in sight. It and **every enemy next to it** take **1d4** (1d6 with Squall) and are thrown straight back from Yorin **2 tiles** (3 with Squall; **1 when it sputters**), the far ones first. Shield Drive's rules for the rest: into a wall or a body, **+1d6**; toward a pit or a drop, a save to catch the lip; every squadmate a thrown enemy leaves gets a free swing; bosses, the immortal and the very heavy (45+ health) take the damage and stagger, unmoved.
- **The Twins** (`b4_twins`, Tallow; free, once a fight): Tallow or a squadmate within **4** carries the Twins until the end of the **next round**. The first blow they **miss** is rolled again (the whole attack, once); the first save they **fail** against **fire** (a burner's flames) or a **daze** is rolled again. Oponn's luck, not sorcery: never sputters. Tallied as `b4Twins`.
- **Autumn's Touch** (`b4_rot`, Hessa; a prayer): an enemy within **2** in sight, no roll: **1d4 + the level's damage** rot, and its armour **−3 through its next two rounds**. Recharges like Shield bash (every other round at Book Four's levels).
- **The Worm Gives Back** (`b4_wormgift`, Hessa; a prayer): Hessa or a hurt squadmate **next to her** heals **1d6 + 2 + (level − 1)** and stops bleeding. **Twice a fight.**

## The Dust and otataral (rulings)

- **Yorin is subject to the Dust.** Serc is a warren: both spells cost strain, so 70's sputter applies (5% a point of Dust: Sky-Fire at half, the Shove throws 1 tile). The sputter now halves `B.warren.serc` alongside Denul and Meanas. The Leper and the grandmother's boon keep him clean as they keep everyone. Tested at Dust 3.
- **Mirrel is subject to the Dust** exactly as Marrow is (the same three spells).
- **Hessa's prayers are not.** D'rek's is a god's answer to a prayer, not a warren, like spirit-craft: no strain, never sputters (tested at Dust 3). Claude's call, as David's brief recommended.
- **Otataral** (`def.nomagic`): Hessa's prayers are marked `sorcery`, so a fight on otataral silences them (canon: otataral deadens a god's power too). Yorin's and Mirrel's spells cost strain and are silenced as every warren is. The Twins are luck, and work.
- **Rasp's munitions** are not sorcery and never sputter; Dust shortens her crossbow like any shot.
