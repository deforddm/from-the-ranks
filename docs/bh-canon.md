**Sources:**

## 1. Shape of the novel

- **Chapters:** 24, plus a Prologue and an Epilogue. Book One opens at ch 1, Book Two at ch 7, Book Three at ch 12, Book Four at ch 17 (W) [1].
- **Chapter 7** is the longest Erikson has written (W, trivia).
- **When:** the Prologue is set 24 days after Sha'ik's execution (W Prologue). Wp says the novel opens two months after *House of Chains*. Both fit: the Prologue comes first, the pursuit after it. Writers say "weeks after Raraku" and never print a year.
- **Two threads.** The Fourteenth runs from the Lato Odhan to Y'Ghatan, the Kokakal shore and Malaz City. Onearm's Host runs from Ehrlitan to G'danisban and Hanar Ara. The two armies never meet (W, every chapter read) [1].

## 2. The Fourteenth's thread (game Ch 1, 3, 5, 7)

- **ch 3, the pursuit:** across the Lato Odhan. The army learns the rebels are bound for Y'Ghatan. Nil and Nether hold a ritual outside camp, and Bottle brings them back (W3) [1].
- **ch 4–6, Leoman takes Y'Ghatan:**
  - He kills the Falah'd Vedor at the gate, makes himself Falah'd, and names Dunsparrow his Third (W4) [1].
  - He puts thirty officers to death for what the garrison had become (W6) [1].
  - He sends the citizens to **Lothal** with their goods, and seizes the olive oil in the *maethgara* (W6) [1].
  - The rebels desecrated **the barrows of the Malazan dead** outside the walls, as seen by Keneb, Temul and Gall (W6) [1].
- **ch 5, the camp before the city:** reinforcements arrive with **Captain Faradan Sort**, late of the Stormwall at Korelri (W5, R5) [H]. Hellian and Urb are reassigned to the 14th (R5) [1]. Blistig tells Keneb the officers mean to confront the Adjunct, and Keneb says wait (W5, R5) [H]. The Adjunct tells Keneb that Dujek's spirit is broken and the Host won't help (W6) [1].
- **ch 7, the assault:**
  - **The plan:** the assault goes in at dusk. Tene Baralta and Keneb lead, **Blistig commands the reserves**, and the Wickans and Khundryl Burned Tears watch for breakouts (W7) [1].
  - **The breach:** the wall goes down to munitions. The marines lead, with the heavies close behind. They are ambushed and push on, and Keneb's and Baralta's soldiers follow (W7) [1].
  - **The trap:** the walls and houses are full of oil. Fanatics fire themselves and the buildings with **nearly 3,000 Malazans, a third of the 14th**, inside. The marines strip off armour in the heat (W7) [1].
  - **The escape:** Sinn makes a path through the fire, with mages outside helping, and **791** soldiers come out by it (W7) [1].
  - **Inside:** the surviving squads reach the temple, Corabb and the children; Bottle follows a rat to a way under the ruins (W7) [1].
  - **After:** Tene Baralta is badly burned. **The army marches for Sotka the next night because of the plague.** Sinn is arrested, and Faradan Sort breaks her out (W7) [1].
- **ch 10, the marines under the city:** they come out after the army has gone. Faradan Sort rebuilds the squads. Hellian's squad gains Balgrid, Lutes, Maybe and Tavos Pond, and Urb becomes a sergeant. Koryk takes bones from the paupers' pits (W10, R10) [H].
- **ch 13, the Sotka Road and the shore:** the survivors march on the Sotka Road to rejoin the army (W13). The army waits **near the Kokakal Sea** for the transport fleet. Blistig fears the fleet will need to land elsewhere, and Tavore won't move. Former rebels now honour Coltaine's memory (W13) [1].
- **ch 16, the loading:**
  - The 14th loads **Admiral Nok's fleet** on the Kokakal shore, two days from sailing. **Grub** warns that leaving in two days drowns them two days past **Sepik Island**, and Keneb pulls his soldiers off the ships for a day (W16) [1].
  - **Temul** says most of the Wickans will leave the army, ashamed at being denied a death in battle (W16) [1].
  - **The Y'Ghatan survivors walk in**, with Quick Ben, Kalam and Fiddler (W16) [1].
- **ch 18, at sea:**
  - The **Silanda** goes to Gesler, with Fiddler's and Balm's squads (W18) [1].
  - On the **Froth Wolf**, Tavore's flagship, the fleet sails into an ambush: Edur lines to the north and south. Quick Ben's "wall of earth" against their wall of water turns them; it was illusion at first, then something more (W18) [1].
- **ch 19:** **Sepik** is deserted, its harbour wrecked; the Edur had found Edur slaves there. No landfall (W19) [1].
- **ch 20:**
  - The Nemil supply ships run for home (W20) [1].
  - **The Perish Grey Helms** meet the fleet in great warships. Destriant Run'Thurvian boards (W20) [1].
  - Tene Baralta is visited by Gethol, Herald of Chains (W20) [1].
- **ch 21:** the fleet goes through **the Realm of Togg and Fanderay** (icebergs, roaring water, ships that seem to fly) rather than past Shal-Morzinn (W21) [1].
- **ch 22:** the **Jakatakan Fleet** meets them, under Commander Exent Hadar, with a Claw among his marines and open hostility to Nil and Nether (W22) [1].
- **ch 23, Malaz City:**
  - **The pogrom:** the city turns on its Wickans. Fist Aragan is new. Captain Rynag is complicit, and Koryk shoots him (W21, W23) [1].
  - **The jetty:** the marines hold the main jetty against the mob, and **Keneb turns the army's allegiance** (W23) [1].
  - **The docks:** the Perish hold the centre docks, and Cuttle clears the jetty with cussers on ballista bolts (W23) [1].
  - **Mock's Hold:** Laseen, Mallick Rel and Korbolo Dom. Korbolo is named **First Sword**, the Red Blades under Tene Baralta break with the Fourteenth, and the Wickans and Khundryl are demanded as the price (W23) [1].
  - **The streets:** Kalam's run, T'amber's death and Fiddler's rescue of the Adjunct (W23) [1].
  - **The departure:** the fleet sails before Nok's ships can engage, and Cartheron Crust brings Grub, Lostara, Sandalath and Withal out (W23) [1].

## 3. The Host's thread (game Ch 2, 4, 6)

- **ch 1, Ehrlitan:** a tavern-keeper takes Apsalar for one of Dujek's army (W1), so the Host is at or near Ehrlitan [1]. Wp: Onearm's Host "lands on the north coast of Seven Cities to finish subduing the rebellion, despite a deadly plague" [H with W1].
- **ch 6, the plague begins:** at Kansu, Paran tells Apsalar the plague is spreading and the priests of D'rek, the foremost healers, have been found slaughtered (W6) [1]. Hellian's prologue at D'rek's temple in Kartool fits (W Prologue).
- **ch 8–13:** Paran crosses plague country south toward Raraku, where villages and hamlets are devastated (W8) [1].
- **ch 13, the camp before G'danisban:**
  - **The place:** the Host is camped **in front of G'danisban**, east of Raraku, in heavy rain (W13) [1].
  - **Paran's return:** Paran is taken for a deserter. He knocks out **Captain Sweetcreek** (a Falari princess) and finds Dujek. **Hurlochel** asks him to take command, and he does so as **"Captain Kindly"**, a name the soldiers know but no one in the Host has met (W13) [1]. The real Captain Kindly is in the Fourteenth with Lieutenant Pores (W13, W16).
- **ch 15:**
  - **The field hospital:** Dujek is dying of the plague. **Fist Rythe Bude** attends him, and **Noto Boil** tries to keep Paran out (W15, R15) [H].
  - **Poliel's temple:** Paran strikes at the temple in G'danisban. Brokeface's mob takes him to Poliel. He pierces her hand with an otataral shard and she dies, with the Deragoth in at the end (W15, R15) [H].
  - **The camp:** Shadowthrone's Hounds run through camp and city (R15) [1].
  - **Soliel:** at her temple, she agrees to heal through the carriers, Noto first (W15, R15) [H].
  - **The vote:** Dujek dies "two bells" before Paran returns, and the army votes Paran **High Fist**, unanimous on the strength of Dujek's logs (W15, R15) [H].
- **ch 16:** **Ormulogun** paints the walls of Dujek's tomb outside G'danisban. Paran puts on Onearm's armour with a new sleeve, and the whole army waits outside the tent (W16) [1].
- **ch 20:** the Host marches **north-east of G'danisban**. Sweetcreek complains, and Rythe Bude defends Paran. **A dozen bright lights fall in a curve below the moon.** Paran halts the column, and with Ormulogun's half-made Deck opens a gate to Mael through Hood's realm (W20) [1].
- **ch 21:** **Hanar Ara, the City of the Fallen.** Mathok's rebels meet the Host outside it and surrender, and Paran lets them go. Felisin Younger is Sha'ik Reborn there, under Kulat (W14, W21) [1].

## 4. Who is where (the squads' margins)

- **The 9th Legion (Blistig's)** holds the reserve at Y'Ghatan (W7). So the squad can be on the reserve line when the fire starts. It goes in, or pulls men out of the gate, without standing in any scene the novel names.
- **Sinn's path** is the 791's way out. The squad leaves by it, never by the tunnel under the city.
- **The Host's Seventh Company** (the Fourth's, from Book Three) is in camp before G'danisban. It is never in the field hospital, the temples or the command tent.
- **The Kokakal shore** is a long camp with the fleet at anchor, so the squad's loading is its own.
- **At sea,** the squad is on a transport, never the Froth Wolf or the Silanda.
- **In Malaz City,** the squad holds a stretch of the waterfront or goes into the streets after the Wickans. It is never in Mock's Hold, the Centre District, Raven Hill or the Mouse.

## 5. Who lives and dies (what the squad may see)

- **Truth** dies at the palace gate (W7); heard of, never seen.
- **Dujek Onearm** dies of the plague (W15); the squad hears the bell and stands for the vote.
- **Poliel** dies in her temple (W15); unseen. Rumour says the goddess is dead.
- **T'amber** dies at the centre docks (W23); unseen.
- **Kalam:** the Fourteenth thinks him lost (W23). **Keep open:** what Shadowthrone did with him.
- **Quick Ben:** lost to the Fourteenth (W23). **Keep open.**
- **Tene Baralta** survives, burned, and goes with the Red Blades (W7, W23).
- **The 3,000 in the fire:** headline only. The barrow outside the walls is the squad's.

## 6. Hard limits (binding)

The squad never stands in, at or aboard:
- the Imperial Warren or a Skykeep;
- Y'Ghatan's palace gate, the temple with Corabb and the children, the tunnels under the city, or the warren of D'riss;
- Paran's sickroom visit, Poliel's temple, Soliel's temple, Dujek's tomb, or the gate to Mael;
- the Silanda or the Froth Wolf;
- Mock's Hold, the Centre District gauntlet, Admiral Bridge, Raven Hill Park, the Mouse, the Hanged Man, Smiley's, Fiddler's tenement, the Deadhouse, or the chamber of the First Throne.

It never speaks with Tavore, Paran, Dujek, Keneb, Blistig, Quick Ben, Kalam, Apsalar, Fiddler, Gesler, Hellian, Faradan Sort, Sinn, Leoman, Corabb, Mathok or Felisin Younger. It may see them at a distance, or hear them through a tent wall.

## 8. The plague (the book's system, Fever)

- Poliel sent it, bowed to the Crippled God, across Seven Cities. "It did not take everyone": survivors, "marked by blood and rot", were drawn to Hanar Ara (W12) [1].
- Carriers spread it; one is a boy with a pack of dogs (W14). Headline only.
- After ch 15, Soliel's healing works through the carriers. Noto Boil is the first (W15, R15) [H]. So Fever's cures before Chapter 4's end are surgeons and Denul; after it, a carrier's touch as well.
- The warrens are "infested with the plague" (W16) [1], so mages pay a "yes, but" when they reach for a warren.

## 9. Gods (who may answer)

- Book Three's and Four's patrons stand. **Poliel never answers.** **Soliel** is stronger from Chapter 4 on (W15).
- **Hood** answers as before; his fall is in *Toll the Hounds*.
- **The Queen of Dreams** is Leoman's ally in this book (W7). If she answers for a squadmate, she never helps against Leoman.
- **Togg and Fanderay's realm** carries the fleet (W21). Their boon stands.

## 10. Timeline (writers only)

- 1164 BS (W Prologue). Book Six runs after Book Four. Books Two and Three are earlier the same year or the year before. Writers never print it.
- Two months after *House of Chains* at the pursuit (Wp).
- Y'Ghatan to the Kokakal sailing: the army marches the next night (W7). The loading waits for the survivors, so allow some weeks [O].

## 13. Where the books meet

## 14. Claude's calls from this sheet

- The Fourteenth's squad is in Blistig's 9th on the reserve line at Y'Ghatan (W7). Its mortal fight starts when the reserve is sent in to hold Sinn's path open. That is an invention in the gap, and changes no canon outcome.
