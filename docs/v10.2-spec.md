# v10.2 spec: soak, wards, death checks, the second stat

These come from the GURPS conversation of Nov 6. David's calls:
- Soak stays small, and munitions, sorcery and pierce attacks ignore it.
- A failed death check wounds and never kills, and there are no death checks in mortal fights (3:25 PM PST).
- Wards come from overheal on the existing heals, plus a few new dark-warren pieces, and **wards never stack**.
- Active defence, fatigue and quirks are parked (`open-questions.md` Q25).

Every number below is a starting point. The all-books balance check after the build tunes them, and any change goes to David first.

## 1. Soak

A hit that lands loses its soak in damage, down to a minimum of 1.

**Who has soak:**

| Who | Soak |
|---|---|
| Squadmates with base armour 15+ (the heavies: Brisk, Haddat, Ramin, the Grey Sword, Hessa, Darrut, Thegg, Ottar) | 1 |
| Body armour of armour 3 (an officer's scale, Aren Guard mail, a Dogslayer's scale, the Finadd's Edur mail, Bluerose scale, the village's old shield) | +1 for the wearer |
| Enemies with armour 16 to 17 (about 30 of 326) | 1 |
| Enemies with armour 18+ and K'Chain Che'Malle | 2 |
| **Cap, for anyone** | 2 |

**What ignores soak:**
- **Munitions:** all of them, opener blasts included.
- **Sorcery:** any warren or spirit ability, Keth's Land's Teeth, Tuft's Meanas, D'rek prayers and the like.
- **Pierce weapons:**
  - the crossbows: the Daru roof-crossbow, the Trygalle crossbow, the Letherii field crossbow, Kettle's cradle, and Tobet's bow if he carries a crossbow (to check);
  - enemy crossbowmen;
  - aimed shots such as the Tracker's Mark.
- **Blows from the flank:** any blow that gets the flanking bonus. The rule is already in the engine: an ally beside the target on the far side.

**Shown:** a small "soak 1" chip on the sheet and on a foe's info. The combat log reads "the blow skids off his scale (−1)".

## 2. Wards and wound-bargains

**The overheal ward.** Healing past full health becomes a ward.
- **Size:** a ward is capped at 25% of the target's max health, rounded.
- **No stacking (David):** a squadmate holds at most one ward. A new ward replaces the old one only if it is bigger. It never adds on.
- **What it does:** it absorbs damage after soak. Pierce and sorcery do not get past it; it is warren-made too. When the ward runs out, the rest of the hit goes to health.
- **When it ends:** at the end of the fight. It carries across a fight's two areas, as wounds and strain already do.
- **Which heals make one:** every heal that targets a squadmate:
  - Denul Wash and Stanch;
  - Salve;
  - Rally;
  - the Spirit-Song of the Herds;
  - The Worm Gives Back;
  - the group-heal tricks;
  - the gods' healing boons (Soliel, the ground, the Hold).
  - The Old Woman's Bargain and the Shield Anvil's Weight keep their own shape, and their overheal becomes a ward too.
- **Flavour:** one log line per warren, and nothing else changes on screen:
  - Denul: "clean skin closes over where the next cut will be";
  - D'rek: "earth-dark, like dried mud";
  - Soliel's oil: "warm";
  - the spirits: "the ground stays close".
- **Shown:** a pale band over the health bar.

**New pieces (few, Claude's calls, David may cut any):**
1. **A Hood-touched charm** (an item, Book Eight's first find, or backfilled to a Book Six barrow). Once a fight, it takes a squadmate's wound and leaves it on the nearest enemy below half health. It is the Bargain's shape, with Hood's taste for the dying.
2. **A Kurald Galain shroud** (a trick for a Tiste Andii-touched scene, Book Eight, Black Coral). Once a fight, a squadmate's ward is made at full size (25%) at once, with no heal needed. The darkness takes the next blow.
3. **Book Eight's tricks and healers** are designed with wards and bargains from the start.

## 3. Death checks (revised: David, 3:25 PM PST)

**Not in mortal fights.** A mortal fight keeps today's rule: everyone down at its end is dead, and the sergeant takes a scar instead.

**Everywhere else**, at 0 health a squadmate is **down and out for the rest of the fight**, as today. Healing still cannot reach a downed squadmate; only Argument with Hood, once a fight, stands one up, as now. The one exception is a natural 20 on a death check.

**The roll:** at the start of each of their turns they roll a d20, plus 1 at level 8 and 1 more at level 16. They need 10 or more on Soldier, 11 on Bridgeburner.
- **Natural 20:** they get up at 1 health and fight on (David, 3:28 PM PST). The only way a check brings anyone back.
- **Natural 1:** counts as two failures.
- **Three successes:** steady. They stop rolling, still down, with no wound.
- **Three failures:** a **wound** (below). They stay down.
- **Story:** no rolls and no wounds.

**Stabilise:** a squadmate next to a downed one spends their action, and the downed one stops rolling with no wound. They are still down. The cost is the helper's turn in the fight. This is a new button, shown only when someone is down nearby.

**Wounds:**
- A named injury, for example a cracked rib, a deep cut, a bad knee or a ringing head. Each is −3 max health or −1 to hit.
- It lasts until the chapter's next fire, where a healer or a rest clears it.
- It is shown on the sheet and named in the log ("Haddat will be carrying that rib a while").
- A squadmate carries at most one. A second failure replaces the first with the worse one.

So death checks mostly add a cost: a wound you carry, or a turn spent keeping a friend from one. The natural 20 is the one lucky break, at 1 health; a squadmate who is stabilised stops rolling and gives up that chance. Mortal fights are unchanged.

## 4. The second stat

Today a squadmate's main stat grows at levels 4 and 12, and the second at 8. **New:** the second also grows at 6 and 10. That gives +3 by level 10, against the main's +2 by 12.

Books One, Two and Five cap at 8, so they get +2 there. Every other book starts at 8 or later, so they have it at once from a transfer or a fresh start.
