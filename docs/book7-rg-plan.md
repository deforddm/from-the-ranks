# Book Seven: beside *Reaper's Gale* (plan)

**Book Seven at a glance:**
- abbreviation `rg`, ids `b7_`;
- shelf `src/120`–`139`;
- version v10.0.0, branch `book7`.

The series plan gives Book Seven two squads, both reached by a transfer from a road built for the purpose:
- the Third on Book Six's `adjunct` road;

The Fourth stays out: the Host doesn't go to Lether, so its next book is Book Eight. *Reaper's Gale* is the book where the Malazans invade Lether. So this is where the two squads stand on opposite sides of one war.

## Questions for David (a recommendation first in each)

1. **How the two squads share the book.**
   - **(a) Two threads, taking turns, the way Book Six does: recommended.** The Third plays Chapters 1, 3, 5 and 7: the landing, the march inland, the push on Letheras. The Letherii squad plays Chapters 2, 4 and 6: Letheras under the Edur and the Patriotists, then the army sent out to meet the invaders. Both squads stand on the field before Letheras in the last battle, on opposite sides, and never cross blades on the page. That last part is a recommendation of its own. The alternative is to keep them apart, with the Letherii squad held inside the city walls.
   - (b) The Third only, with the Letherii as news and rumour.
   - (c) The Letherii only, with the Malazans as the enemy coming up the roads.
2. **Transfers and a fresh start.**
   - The new-sergeant screen offers up to two saves: a finished Book Six save on the `adjunct` road, and a finished Book Five save on the `emperor` road. Either can be left empty.
   - A transfer carries level, gear, picks, tricks and the roster, as every book does.
   - A fresh start gets each squad's canon-default survivors.
   - Book Six's `jetty` and `wickans` roads, and Book Five's `paid` and `ledger` roads, are heard of as rumour.
3. **Levels: each squad keeps its own (recommended).**
   - The Third ends Book Six at up to 18. A fresh start is at 16, and the cap is 21.
   - The Letherii end Book Five at up to 8. A fresh start is at 8, and the cap is 12.
   - The fights in each squad's chapters are built for that squad's level.
   - This needs a per-squad cap in the engine, a small change. The alternative is to lift the Letherii to the Third's level, which their story doesn't earn.
4. **The book's own system: the Rising (recommended).**
   - Tavore's plan is to fight the Edur and set the Letherii against them: civil war, the Indebted rising (R13 [1]).
   - One number, the Rising, counts how far Lether has turned. The Third raises it by how it treats towns, the Indebted and prisoners; the Letherii squad by what it does or won't do for the Patriotists.
   - A high Rising helps the Third: towns open, guides walk in, supplies are found. It hunts the Letherii squad: their file at the Patriotists grows, informers listen, and a check can turn into an arrest.
   - That gives one system with two faces, and the two threads pull on it together.
   - The alternative is **Supply**: the marines live off the land with no line behind them. It fits the Third only.
5. **Permanent deaths: one signposted mortal fight (recommended), in Chapter 5.**
   - The Third, cut off in a village on the road to Letheras, is caught by an Edur column. Canon has a fight like it, with named squads (R21). Ours is a different village, the same day, and never the canon squads' fight.
   - Option: a second mortal fight for the Letherii in Chapter 6, the battle before Letheras. That would be a first, two in one book. Say if you want it.
6. **Title, flavour line, patch.**
   - Title: ***Unwitnessed***, recommended. Tavore's word to the army: what the marines do on Lether no one will see or sing (R17 [1]). Other choices: *The Burned Ships* (they burn their transports on landing, R13); *Debts Called In*.
   - Flavour line: "The Malazans burn their ships on a hostile shore. In the capital, the Letherii who survived the Edur learn to fear their own neighbours. One war, two squads, opposite sides."
   - Patch: the Third's patch gains a burned ship's prow; the Letherii company's patch gains the Edur's mark struck through. The rules are in `series-patches.md`.
7. **Recruits** (up to six on the roster), recommended:
   - **The Third:** an Indebted farmhand who walks into the marines' camp after they burn a factor's ledger (Chapter 1). A Shake fisher from the coast, one of Twilight's people, who knows the inland roads (Chapter 3).
   - **The Letherii:** a Patriotist clerk who wants out (Chapter 2). A Bluerose rider of the cavalry (Chapter 4).
   - Book Six's and Book Five's recruits come across on a transfer if they finished on the roster.
8. **The roads out (three a squad).**
   - **The Third:**
     - `adjunct`: stays with the Bonehunters in Letheras. This road leads to Book Nine, *Dust of Dreams*.
     - `letheras`: takes the new king's coin, and a post in the city.
     - `home`: time served, a berth home.
   - **The Letherii:**
     - `army`: in the Letherii army under the new order. Canon sends a Letherii army with the Bonehunters into the Wastelands in *Dust of Dreams* (to be checked at the canon sheet), so this road leads to Book Nine.
     - `palace`: guards for the new king.
     - `free`: debts forgiven, out of the army.
