**src/031_battle.js**
- hurt(): the difficulty scaling comes first, then soak (`u.soakNext`, which attack() sets for the one blow and then clears), then the ward, then everything that was already there.
- heal(): turns overheal into a ward.
- attack(): works out `past` (flank, sorcery, aimed or pierce). It adds `[sorcery]`, `[aimed]` or `[pierces]` to the log tag; flanking already had its own `flanking +2` tag. It hands soak to hurt().
- mkParty: sets `u.soak` and applies a carried wound. mkFoe: sets soak, pierceShot and sorcShot. mkAlly: sets pierceShot and sorcShot (allies have no soak).

- useAb() sets `B.healSrc` to the ability's key while it runs.
- nextTurn(): a downed squadmate rolls the death check at the start of their turn, and a natural 20 goes on into an ordinary turn. Argument with Hood resets the count.
- The squad's AI turn (the play bot, and the balance bot's naive runs) stabilises a downed friend when one is beside it.
- UI: soak, ward and wound chips in the unit bar; soak and ward in the tap-info lines; a Stabilise button, only shown when it can be used; the keyboard hint counts it; a pale ward band on the health bar.

## What counts as what

- **Soak applies only to blows and shots that go through attack().** Damage that goes straight to hurt() never meets soak: blasts (every munition, the opener, Quorl, Blue Fire, the bola, the lamp, pitch pots), burning, falls, slams, and foes' special effects such as drain, chill, the Stonebound slam and the Tyrant's lance. (Claude's call: it is the simplest line that gives "munitions and sorcery ignore it". It also means a few physical specials, such as a slam, ignore soak.)
- **Sorcery** (goes past soak):
  - Tuft's shadow-lash, her ordinary attack whenever otataral is not in force;
  - any blow with `ground:true` (Keth's Land's Teeth, the Book Four lightning);
  - the Semk god's fire (`sorc:true`);
  - foes whose ordinary blow is sorcery (`FOE_SORC`): clawmage, b5_krisnan, b7_krisnan, b7_lethmage, b3_c6_ridden, b3_c6_greymage, b3_c7_greymage;
  - every other warren, spirit or prayer ability (strain, `sorcery`, `spirit` or `prayer` abilities, D'rek's prayers among them) does its damage through hurt() or blast(), so it never meets soak anyway.
- **Pierce** (goes past soak):
  - a squadmate's own basic attack at range with the Daru roof-crossbow, the Trygalle crossbow or the Letherii field crossbow equipped;
  - Kettle's crossbow and cradle (her basic ranged attack);
  - enemy crossbowmen: any foe or ally whose key or name says crossbow or xbow, or whose blow is a quarrel or a bolt, except the sorcery list. That is 21 kinds, among them xbow, guildbow, b7_malxbow, b7_lethxbow, b3_moranth and Fiddler.
  - Tobet carries a bow, not a crossbow, so he pierces only with one of the three crossbows equipped. (Spec item "to check": checked.)
- **Aimed:** every hit on a target under the Tracker's Mark.
- **Flank:** any blow that gets the +2 flanking bonus (atkCalc's `fl`).
- **K'Chain Che'Malle:** found by name (K'ell, K'Chain, Che'Malle, Ve'Gath). That is b3_khell, b3_c4_roped and b3_c7_hurthunter. The K'ell Hunter's hide (`dr`) still applies as before, on top of its soak.

## Claude's calls (David may overturn any)

1. Foe soak is read from the foe's base armour in FOES, before the difficulty and the B*GAP armour rows (the spec says soak is armour, not difficulty). It counts 29 foes at 1, plus raest, lorn and the three K'Chain at 2.
2. Allies have no soak (the spec names only squadmates and enemies). Their crossbows and sorcery do go past enemy soak.
3. Crits do not go past soak (the spec doesn't say). The K'ell hide's crit rule is unchanged.
4. The soak log line is "The blow skids off X's scale/mail/shield/armour (−N)" for the squad, and "X's armour/hide" for foes. Tags: `[sorcery]`, `[aimed]`, `[pierces]`.
5. The ward is shown as a "ward N" chip in the unit bar, as part of the tap-info line, and as a pale band drawn over the left of the health bar.
6. A ward rides in `S.bopt.wards` only between the two areas of one fight, the way the carried health already does, so a reload in the second area restores it. win() drops it, and nothing else saves it.
7. The ward's flavour depends on the source:
   - Denul: Mend, Wash, Stanch;
   - D'rek: b4_rot, b4_wormgift;
   - Soliel: the boon's oil;
   - spirits: the ground boon, the Rhivi Spirit-Song, and any ability marked `spirit` (the Herds' song, the Old Woman's Bargain, the Shield Anvil);
   - plain: everything else, Salve and Rally among them.
   A heal that lands later on a timer reads as plain.
8. The five wounds, in order of how bad they are: a deep cut (−3 health), a ringing head (−1 to hit), a bad knee (−1 to hit), a cracked rib (−3 health), a wrenched shoulder (−1 to hit). They are stored as `S.injury[id]`, because `S.wounds` is already Bridgeburner's carried health. A second wound keeps the worse of the two.
9. Wounds clear at any chapter fire (a ROSTER_FIRES node), on any rest(), and at the start of a new chapter. Most Book One chapters have no fire, so that last one matters there.
10. A natural 20 with no free tile to stand on counts as a success.
11. On a natural 20 the squadmate gets up and acts that turn. The count starts again if they go down again.
12. Death-check log lines are "fights for breath: holds on / slips (N held, N slipped)", plus steady, wound and up lines. The dice show when SET.dice is on.
13. The play bot stabilises whenever a downed friend is beside it, and spends its whole turn on it. The balance bot does it right after Argument with Hood.
14. Backup edge: it applies to bare checks (the minigames) as well as story checks, since neither is a trick check.
