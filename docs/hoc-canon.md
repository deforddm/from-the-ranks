Book Four is Book Two's squad, folded into the Adjunct's Fourteenth Army as medium infantry of Blistig's Ninth Legion, marching from Aren to Raraku. ## 1. The campaign, in order (game Chapters 1–4)

### 1.1 Game Chapter 1, The Muster: Aren (novel ch 8)

- **Where.** The muster is on Aren's marshaling ground (also called the assembly ground). [H] W8, R8
  - Other places: the barracks and the yard behind them, where Pella drills with bones; Lostara's room; the review ground; the *Silanda*, impounded in the harbour. [H] W8, R8
  - A cemetery near the barracks has bones lying open; it is where the soldiers' bones come from. [H] W8, R8
- **When.** Tavore asks for two days; the army marches in two days. [H] W8, R8
- **Who is there.** [H] W8, R8
  - The Adjunct Tavore. Fists Gamet, Tene Baralta and Blistig.
  - Nil and Nether. Captain Keneb, Lieutenant Ranal and Grub.
  - Strings (Fiddler), Cuttle, Sergeants Borduke and Gesler, Stormy, Truth and Pella.
  - Pearl and Lostara Yil.
  - T'amber is named, not seen: Gamet thinks she was a concubine at the Queen of Dreams' Grand Temple on Unta.
- **The review.** Tavore reviews a Fourteenth in disorder. Gamet's 8th Legion, about 4,000, is the first legion drawn up, and it is late. Admiral Nok has already sailed with the fleet. [H] W8, R8
- **Cuttle's blast.** Cuttle comes in late with about a dozen ragged men just out of the cells. He had broken the neck of the noble Lenestro in revenge for the Chain of Dogs. At Strings's signal he sets Moranth munitions under the cobbles; the blast knocks a third of the legion flat and order comes back. He turns down Gamet's offer of a command, stays with Strings, and says he has never met him. [H] W8, R8
  - *For Book Two's players:* Lenestro is a Book Two name (DGC, the Nobles' Council). He dies here at Cuttle's hands, offstage.
- **The omen.** Grub, a small boy, walks onto the Adjunct's review ground holding a sun-bleached human thighbone. Keneb takes him up and says Duiker gave Grub to him. Tavore: "It is far too late for that." The soldiers read the bone as a death omen. [H] R8, W8
- **"Swallow it whole."** Strings gathers the veterans and tells the story of Admiral Nok and the snake barrel. [H] W8, R8
  - The squads raid the graveyard, and the soldiers begin wearing finger bones.
  - Ranal blames Strings.
  - Tavore orders the bones worn the same way by everyone, orders Ranal to wear one, and orders Gamet to make a standard from them.
- **The barracks.** Tavore confines the troops to barracks, and the **Red Blades** seize the drink and the durhang. Blistig calls her mad and says the army is finished. He and Baralta suspect Keneb of staging Grub's walk-on. [H] R8, W8
- **Pearl and Lostara.** Tavore has sent them after Felisin. They question Gesler and Stormy, and Stormy throws Pearl across the room. Pella admits he helped Felisin, Heboric and Baudin escape Skullcup, with Kulp's help. Pearl reads the *Silanda* and its Tiste Edur and Tiste Andii dead. [H] R8, W8
- **The Wickans.** Three ships bring 300 Wickans of the Crow Clan, "Coltaine's own", as volunteers. [H] R8, W8
- **Not in it.** No riot (Cuttle's blast is the nearest thing). No Deck reading. No deaths on the page except Lenestro, offstage. Blistig talks of mutiny only as a prediction. [1] R8

### 1.2 Game Chapter 2, Coltaine's Fall: the Aren Way (novel ch 11)

- **Where.** Coltaine's Fall: the hill on the Aren Way, covered in ants and in memorials (braids, dog skulls, crow feathers). [H] R11, W11
  - The road is lined with spiked trees hung with fetishes and painted soldier figures. Tavore forbids cutting them down. [H] R11, W11
- **When.** The end of the first day out. Gamet notes they have barely moved. [1] R11
- **Who is there.** [H] R11, W11
  - Gamet, Tavore and Tene Baralta. Baralta names the Khundryl and the Semk among the clans who fought at the Fall.
  - Fiddler, Gesler, Borduke and Cuttle.
  - Keneb and Ranal, who ask for a list of the mages.
  - The mages Bottle, Tavos Pond and Balgrid.
  - Temul.
  - Clawmaster Topper.
  - T'amber, whom Gamet is sent to fetch.
- **The standard** is raised: "a thin figure holding up a bone before a sandstorm". [H] R11, W11
- **The marines' mages.** Strings's squad is Koryk, Tarr, Smiles, Bottle and Cuttle. The Meanas mages are Bottle (Strings's squad), Tavos Pond (Gesler's) and Balgrid (Borduke's). [H] R11, W11
  - *Conflict:* the W *Borduke* page puts Tavos Pond in Borduke's squad.
- **Temul.** Gamet reports that the Crow elders refuse Temul as their commander. [H] R11, W11
- **Topper's news.** Tavore seals her otataral sword in a stone box. Topper comes through a portal with news of Genabackis: Whiskeyjack is dead, the Bridgeburners and Paran are dead (as Topper believes), and Dujek is broken. He says desertions may follow; Tavore will not allow them. [H] R11, W11
  - The detail of what Topper says is being re-checked (§2).
- **That night.** [H] R11, W11, except as marked
  - Bottle listens through small creatures and spreads the news.
  - Fiddler grieves, and finds Temul weeping. He tells him to take the Wickans' horses to humble them (R11 only; the W *Temul* page leaves out the horses [1]).
  - Temul shows Fiddler Duiker's scroll. [1] R11
  - Cuttle tells Fiddler that Quick Ben is alive and a High Mage. The two agree the Adjunct is "cold iron".
- **Deaths:** none on the page. [1] R11

### 1.3 Game Chapter 3, The Burned Tears: the Vathar Hills, the Crossing and Ubaryd (novel ch 15)

- **Where.** [H] W15, R15
  - The camp in the **Vathar Hills**.
  - **Vathar Crossing**, a ford choked with bones from the Chain of Dogs' Day of Pure Blood, and with tribal offerings of animal carcasses on poles.
  - A butte north of the river, looking down on **Ubaryd** and its harbour.
- **Who is there.** [H] R15, W15
  - Fiddler and his squad, and Cuttle.
  - Tavore, Gall, Temul, Gamet and Keneb.
  - Gesler, Stormy and Truth.
  - Nil, Nether and Tene Baralta.
  - The sapper Maybe.
  - Roach, a scarred cattle-dog that lived through the Fall.
- **The Khundryl.** [H] R15, W15, except as marked
  - About 3,000 riders shadow the army, and Fiddler's squad goes to look at them.
  - They are Gall's **Khundryl Burned Tears**. They have already destroyed the raiders who were harrying the Fourteenth, so the column was raided before this chapter, off the page.
  - Gall has come to make amends for riding away before Coltaine's last battle.
  - Tavore accepts them. Gall kneels to Temul, and Temul, prompted by Fiddler, takes them into the Crow Clan.
  - The W *Temul* page adds that Gall gave Temul his broken sword, which ended the Wickans' dispute and left Temul about 3,000 at his back. [1]
- **The feast.** That night there is a feast, and the army gets drunk. Gall's shamans foretell "a long but restless life" for the army, a life of searching. Gall calls Vathar Crossing "a place of death". [H] R15, W15
- **Keneb.** He admits he is distrusted for an alleged desertion in the uprising: his family came first. Gamet asks him to watch Gesler, and Gesler's squad is kept in the rearguard. [H] R15, W15
- **Fiddler.** [H] R15, W15, except as marked
  - He regrets re-enlisting, and Gesler talks him round. The W *Fiddler* page says he considered deserting. [1]
  - A presence speaking through Nil and Nether's butterflies tells him Raraku is waiting and his "kin" will join him to kill the goddess: "We are awakened."
- **The crossing.** Fiddler and Cuttle blow the ford clear with crackers and cussers, and Maybe checks for holes. Keneb gives Fiddler's squad the honour of crossing first. [H] R15, W15
- **The butte.** From the butte the army sees Ubaryd retaken by Nok, which promises resupply, and the Whirlwind on the horizon. Tavore wonders about spies in the army. [H] R15, W15
- **Deaths:** none on the page. [1] R15

### 1.4 Game Chapter 4, Leoman's Raids: the column and the hills with barrows (novel ch 18–19)

- **The night raid (ch 18).** Leoman and about 200 warriors hit the Malazan camp with clay balls of lamp oil, thrown like bolas. The real aim is to draw the Malazan horse into a chase, and it works. [H] R18, W18
  - The W *Leoman* page says at least a dozen supply wagons burned. [1]
- **The week.** The epigraph of ch 19 is from Keneb's journals: in the first week of the march, Grub, about five years old, refused a wagon and walked in the year's hottest sun. [1] W19
- **The Seti ambush (ch 19).** Leoman's band ambushes the **Seti**, the Malazans' allied horse, and kills more than 300. Many wounded are left for the Malazans to tend. [H] R19, and W *Leoman* (300 Seti cavalry lured into a gorge)
- **The barrow trap.** Tavore sends the marines out at night in a trap after **Dassem Ultor**. [H] R19, W *Fiddler*
  - First, Fiddler runs a scorpion-betting contest among the 9th Company's three marine squads. His "Joyful Union" splits in two and wins, and his own squad learns who he is and keeps it quiet.
  - Fiddler unpacks a munitions crossbow designed with Hedge and made by a Malaz City jeweller. [1] R19
  - About 800 rebels attack the barrows. The barrows hold empty helms; the marines rise and the munitions fall. Cuttle leaves a sharper in Corabb's lap; Corabb caps it with a helm and is thrown into the air, but lives. [1] R19
- **Gamet's fall.** In fog, Gamet rides toward a Malazan picket that has been overrun. A munition kills the attackers round him and knocks him from his horse, and **Gesler and Pella** pull him out. [H] R19, W *Gamet*
  - The W *Gamet* page says a cusser killed his horse and left him with "his legs shredded and his mind disorientated". [1]
  - He tells Tavore he is not fit to be a Fist and names **Keneb**, who is made acting Fist by field promotion. [H] R19, W *Gamet*, W *Keneb*
- **Leoman.** He sends word to Sha'ik that he will keep raiding and come back three days ahead of Tavore's army. [1] R19
- **Deaths:** more than 300 Seti, and several soldiers at the overrun picket, unnamed. [1] R19

### 1.5 The other side of the Whirlwind, for Chapters 1–4 (context; the squad is never there)

- **Ch 7.** In the Pan'arak oasis camp, Korbolo Dom names his army the **Dogslayers**. Sha'ik has banished **Mallick Rel** and **Pullyk Alar**. [H] W7, R7
  - The council hears the Genabackis news and doubts the "novice" Adjunct.
  - Leoman names the traitors he suspects: L'oric, Dom, Reloe, Henaras and Fayelle. [1] R7
- **Ch 10.** [1] W10
  - Leoman is sent out with a scouting force under orders not to engage, and says he will do as he likes.
  - In Sha'ik's tent the council talks of the new **House of Chains** and the new Unaligned **Master of the Deck**.
  - Dom and Reloe's plan: Febryl kills Sha'ik, Tavore kills Febryl, Dom destroys Tavore, then crushes the rebellion in the Empress's name and takes the throne.
- **Ch 13.** Leoman leaves with about 2,000 (R13; the W *Leoman* page says about 1,500). Heboric is made Treach's Destriant, and his jade hands appear. [H] R13, W13
  - *For the sergeant's god:* Treach answers for the sergeant (plan). Heboric is Treach's in this book, out of sight inside the Whirlwind.
