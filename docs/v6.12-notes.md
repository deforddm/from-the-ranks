# From the Ranks v6.12.0: words said once, and a fight log that scrolls

Sun Oct 18, 2026. David's ask: a pass over the dialogue for conversations that repeat unnecessarily, and a combat log the player can scroll.

## What repeated
`tools/repeat_scan.mjs` renders every node under a few states, takes each branch, comes back the way the branch offers (X → Y → X, and X → Y → Z → X), running each node's own `fx` once as `talk()` does, and reports a hub whose words (200+ characters) come back unchanged. First run: 93 of 2,029 nodes; 37 of those the writers already handle with a revisit flag set in the node's `fx` (the Phoenix Inn, Whiskeyjack's briefing, the wallow); 56 real. Three shapes:
- A hub with branches that come back ("Back to the row"): the rests at the chandler's roof, the ridge, the terrace steps and the quorl hill; the Fourth's fire west of Capustan; the vault under the crossing; Doctor Brack's stall; the quartermasters (every purchase returns to the stall with the whole stall again).
- Pairs of questions that cross-link (Toc's "What happened here?" and "Where's the captain?"; Sethand's dust-line and bundle; Ohl and Brisk at the start of Book Three's Chapter 4): asking the second and then the first again brings the first answer back whole.
- The reader's deck described in full in every chapter's reading offer (`B2RD_DECK`, `B3RD_DECK`): seven times a book.
Seven paragraphs are written into more than one node; all but the deck paragraph are alternatives the player sees one of (a check's pass and fail, the dig's three outcomes).

## The rule (src/16_skill_checks.js, `talk`)
`G.said` keeps, for one sitting of the sheet, the words each node showed (rendered, with counts such as silver blanked). A node shown again with the same words:
- shows its `again` line if the writer gave one (`again:` on the node, a string or a function), or
- shows its words folded to the first line (`.txt.said`, a fade, and a "Read it again" button that unfolds them).
A choice is dropped when its target was heard with the same words and every way out of that target is back here or to a node this page offers anyway (`topicHeard`); never the last choice. The picker's Back un-says the node first, so the words come back whole. A sitting ends when the sheet closes, the map or a fight starts, or a vision plays (`G.said = null`). A node can opt out with `again:false`.

`again` lines written (Claude's call, in each place's voice): c4_close, c5_close, c6_close, c7_close, b3_c4_fire, c6_vault, c3_brack_wares, and the quartermasters pell, c1_pell, b3_c1_pell, b3_c2_qm, b3_c5_qm, b3_c6_wagons, b3_c6_gear (each ending with the silver carried). The deck offers: the reader's deck in full the first time that reader reads in a book (`S.f.b2_rd_<reader>`, `S.f.b3_rd_<reader>`, set by the offer node's `fx`), then `B2RD_AGAIN` / `B3RD_AGAIN`.

## The fight log (src/31_battle.js, src/01_style.css)
`blog` keeps the last 80 lines (was 5), newest first. `.log` is a box with `max-height:7.8rem` (`min(40vh,16rem)` on the wide layout) and `overflow-y:auto`, so the newest line sits at the top and the history scrolls below it. `logFit` no longer hides lines; in a narrow PC window `battleSettle` still measures the room under the turn bar and `logFit` sets it as the box's height.

## Tests
`tools/said_test.mjs` (18 checks): a bare dialogue of its own for the rules (fold, "Read it again", the `again` line, the heard topic dropped, the last choice kept, the picker's Back, the four new-sitting points), the deck offers in both books, and the log (80 kept, the box scrolls, nothing hidden, the narrow-window room). `tools/repeat_scan.mjs` stays as the pass to run on a new book: it marks each hub `again` or `folds`.

## For the writers' brief
A hub the player comes back to in one sitting wants an `again:` line (one sentence, the place and the moment), or it folds. A topic whose only way out is "Back" is dropped once heard; give it another exit if it should stay. The deck's description belongs in the first offer only.
