- `SOLDIER_LATE` in src/31c_stakes.js: per book (`gotm`, `dg`, `moi`) and chapter (3–7), `{hp ×, atk +, dmg +}`. `soldierLate()` returns the entry only when `S.diff` is Soldier; `mkFoe` (src/31_battle.js) applies it on top of everything else. Story and Bridgeburner never see it. Chapters 1–2 have no entry.
- `SOLDIER_SKIP`: Book Three's Coral mortal fights (`b3_c7_hunt`, `b3_c7_stakes`) keep their weight, as David asked.

## Off target, and why

1. **Health lost stays low.** Most chapters still sit at 15–28% lost, under the 30–50% asked (in band: Book One Ch5 and Book Two Ch3). The bot's outcomes are all-or-nothing: tougher foes cut wins long before they raise the cost of a win. Its healers mend through a longer fight; when a fight goes wrong, it is lost, not won dearly. More extra would push wins under 85% first.
2. **Soldier is now harder than Bridgeburner late.** Bridgeburner's foes are ×1.25 health, ×1.2 damage, +1 to hit; the late Soldier extras are bigger (up to ×1.7, +2, +5). The bot wins late Bridgeburner fights 92–100% (it starts each fight at full health, so Bridgeburner's carried wounds don't show), against 79–100% on Soldier now. David should decide: accept it, or raise Bridgeburner's late chapters too (not done: he said Bridgeburner must not change).
3. **Still easy:** Book One Ch7 (100%), Book Two Ch7 (100%), Book Three Ch4 and Ch6 (100%). Book Three Ch4 was cut back after a trial at ×1.05/+2 dropped the breakout to 25–63%.
4. **A little hard:** Book Two Ch6 79% (Sanimon 50%; the same table gave 88% in the trial round, so partly noise), Book Two Ch3 84% (two fights at 75%).
5. **The play bot** (`play.mjs`, 3 runs, Book One) finishes all three runs OK; one run took 740 s, losing and re-fighting Ch3–6 fights several times. `run_all --play=3` will take longer.

Two tuning rounds plus the after pass were run (the lead's limit). The next levers, untested: Book One Ch7 and Book Two Ch7 health ×1.9; Book Three Ch6 ×1.6; Book Two Ch6 back to ×1.35.
