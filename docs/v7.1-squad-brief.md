# v7.1 squad brief: three more soldiers of the Fourteenth

David's decisions (Oct 26, 2026): the Fourteenth's refill is the sergeant's pick at the muster, from a larger pool; three new soldiers join the pool (a Denul healer, a Serc mage, a sapper); Hessa becomes a battle-priest of D'rek; Tallow becomes a skirmisher with a sling and the Twins' luck. Engine detail and numbers: `docs/v7.1-notes.md`.

**For chapter writers.** Any of the seven may be in the squad from Chapter 1 on. `b41New()`…`b47New()` (which read `HOC_NEW`) now include `mirrel`, `yorin` and `rasp`. Where a chapter gives Tallow, Hessa, Tebbet and Darrut a line, give the three new ones a line too, guarded by `b4In(id)`. Rasp is never in a squad with Tangle (the muster does not offer her when he is there), so a line for "the sapper" can be `b4In('tangle') ? … : b4In('rasp') ? … : …`. Mirrel is a healer: where Marrow's lines are about tending the squad and Marrow is absent, Mirrel can take them.

**Tallow and Hessa changed.** Tallow carries a sling now, not a spear: chapter text that has him "with his spear upright" (Ch6 and Ch7, in the recruits' row) needs the sling or his hands instead. Hessa still carries the temple spear and the tall shield; she now prays in a fight, and D'rek answers.

## Mirrel: the healer

- **id** `mirrel`. **She**, 34. **Origin** Li Heng, Quon Tali. **Role** Healer · Denul · Li Heng.
- **Who.** A midwife's daughter from Li Heng who learned Denul in the city's poorhouse hospice, where nobody paid and everybody bled. She signed on when the Fourteenth was raised because the army pays its healers, and this is her first campaign. She has healed a great many people. She has never healed one who was trying to kill somebody else at the time, and is finding that it makes no difference to the work.
- **Voice.** Brisk and plain. Counts in threes ("one, two, three, and up"). Talks to wounds, not to the people they are in. Impatient with anyone who will not sit still, and says so. Dry about the gods. Not old and strange the way Marrow is: young, tidy, certain.
  - "Sit. Hand. Other hand. Good."
  - "The warren does not have moods, Sergeant. People do."
  - "In Li Heng we'd have called this a quiet night."
- **God.** Soliel, Mistress of Healing (reuses Ohl's and Marrow's sigil and boon: "Temple oil").
- **Kit.** A hospice bag with a red hand stitched on it, a roll of clean linen she will not lend, a brass thimble.

## Yorin: the squad mage (Serc)

- **id** `yorin`. **He**, 27. **Origin** Cawn, Quon Tali. **Role** Squad mage · Serc · Cawn.
- **Who.** A barge-master's son from Cawn who could tell the river's weather three days out, and then found out why. Serc, the Path of the Sky. A cadre school took him for a season and sent him back as "unfinished"; the Fourteenth was not particular. He calls lightning and he moves air, both in short supply of patience, and he is frightened of the Whirlwind in a professional way: it is the biggest thing in the sky he has ever seen, and it is not his.
- **Voice.** Quick, nervous, talks about the sky as if it were a difficult relative. Superstitious about dust. Funny when frightened, which is often.
  - "Sky's clear, Sergeant. That's the trouble with it. It never stays clear."
  - "That's not weather out there. Weather has the decency to move."
  - "I can put lightning on him. I can't promise it'll be the same him afterwards."
- **God.** Shadowthrone (a mage has to swear by somebody, he says, and Shadowthrone at least answers mages). Reuses Tuft's sigil and boon: "Shadow wraps the squad".
- **Kit.** A copper weather-vane the size of his palm, a knife, a cadre school's chit that says "unfinished".
- **The Dust.** Serc is a warren: in Book Four's Dust (Chapter 5 on) his spells sputter like any warren-mage's. He knows it, and minds it more than anyone.

## Rasp: the sapper

- **id** `rasp`. **She**, 29. **Origin** Malaz City. **Role** Sapper · marine-trained · Malaz City.
- **Who.** Trained a year at the marine sappers' school on Malaz Island, and passed. The Fourteenth had more trained sappers than marine squads to put them in, so she was posted to the 9th's medium infantry with a satchel and a crossbow, and has decided not to take it personally. She is named for the file on her belt and for her voice, which a cusser went off too near when she was nineteen.
- **Voice.** Hoarse, short, and fond of munitions to a degree the squad finds worrying. She does not count the satchel; she weighs it in her hand and knows. Kind to recruits in a way that sounds like threats.
  - "Don't touch that. Don't look at it like that either."
  - "Moranth clay. Lovely stuff. Smells like the end of an argument."
  - "Marines get the good fuse. I get the fuse the marines dropped. It burns the same."
- **God.** Cotillion, the Rope (every sapper says a word to the Rope before a fuse is lit). Reuses Ellis's sigil and boon: "The Rope".
- **Kit.** The file on her belt, a coil of marine fuse, a Malaz City harbour token for luck.
- **Tangle.** The muster never offers her while Tangle is in the squad: one sapper is a squad's worth.

## Tallow and Hessa, v7.1

- **Tallow** (he, 18): **Skirmisher · Quon Tali**. A sling (range 4, lighter damage) instead of the spear he was told to carry; quicker on his feet. He threw stones at gulls off the Quon coast for ten years and the squad has found a use for it. His grandmother's god, Oponn: **the Twins** (once a fight, a squadmate within 4 gets a second roll at a missed blow or a failed save). Talents: *Grandmother's Luck* (`nerve`), *Keep Moving* (`ghost`, replacing *Hold, Recruit*; an old save's pick moves over by itself).
- **Hessa** (she, 31): **Battle-priest of D'rek · Kartool**. Same spear and shield, nearly the same numbers. *Shield bash* is gone; she has two prayers: **Autumn's Touch** (rot that strips an enemy's armour) and **The Worm Gives Back** (a small heal for herself or a squadmate beside her). D'rek's is a god's answer to a prayer, not a warren: **the Dust never touches it** (like spirit-craft). Otataral does: in a fight on otataral her prayers go unanswered.

## How they sit with everyone (rel)

These are on the character sheets. Writers may use them as seeds.

**Mirrel**
- Haddat: She asked Haddat to hold a man down once, and Haddat did, and neither has mentioned it since. They like each other.
- Sathi: Sathi will not let her near Hackle. Mirrel has stitched Hackle twice, at night, without asking.
- Tangle: Asked him to keep the satchel on the far side of the fire from her linen. He moved it. She counts that as a courtship.
- Marrow: Two Denul healers in one squad. They argue about the warren in whispers, like two cooks sharing a kitchen.
- Ramin: Mended his sword hand and told him to rest it. He has not. She has written his name down.
- Keth: The old woman in him asked to see her hands, and looked at them a long while. Mirrel does not know what she saw.
- Tallow: He comes to her with every scrape. She suspects he likes being told to sit still.
- Hessa: Denul and D'rek. Mirrel heals; Hessa says the Worm will have it all back eventually. They get on better than that sounds.
- Tebbet: Rebinds his bow hand after drill. He thanks her in Dal Honese. She has started answering in Li Heng street cant.
- Darrut: He calls her "the surgeon", which she is not, and she lets him.
- Orvas: He offered to cut his cheek for her, to show her how the tears are done. She said no, firmly, and gave him a lesson in clean cuts instead.
- Yorin: She makes him drink water after every spell. He forgets. She does not.
- Rasp: Rasp's hands are a map of small burns. Mirrel keeps a separate salve for them.

**Yorin**
- Haddat: Haddat asks him, every morning, what the sky will do. He tells her. She believes him, which frightens him.
- Sathi: Sathi says Wickans can smell rain. He has tested her. She can.
- Tangle: Tangle keeps the satchel downwind of him, "in case of lightning". Yorin does not think that is how lightning works. He is not sure.
- Marrow: She calls him "child" and makes him sit down after every spell. He has stopped arguing.
- Ramin: Ramin asks him to keep the lightning away from the horses. He has promised, and means it.
- Keth: The grandmother says his warren is "very loud". He has started casting more quietly round the boy.
- Tallow: Two Quon boys off the transports. They talk about the sea. Neither of them says they miss it.
- Hessa: She says the sky is only the ground's roof. He has not found an answer to that in three weeks.
- Tebbet: Tebbet asks which way the wind will be at the next halt, every halt, for his arrows. He is right more often than Yorin.
- Darrut: Darrut tells him the Aren Guard used to hang mages from the wall for lightning in the harbour. Yorin has decided it is a joke.
- Orvas: Orvas wants to know if he can make a horse run faster with the wind. He can. They have not told the sergeant.
- Mirrel: She makes him drink water after every spell. He forgets. She does not.
- Rasp: Rasp has asked him, with interest, how close lightning has to be to light a fuse. He walks on the other side of the column.

**Rasp**
- Haddat: Haddat walks between her and the recruits on the march. Rasp has decided that is respect, and is right.
- Sathi: Sathi wants to know if a sharper will scare a horse. "Yours, or the other side's?" They are friends.
- Tangle: Has heard of Mincer's sappers. Would like to see his fuse-work. Has decided to stay in a different squad from it, if the Fourteenth can arrange that.
- Marrow: Marrow has looked at her burned hands and said nothing, and given her a tin of salve. Rasp keeps the tin, full.
- Ramin: Ramin treats her like a lady. It confuses her. She has started to enjoy it.
- Keth: The grandmother calls her "the loud one". Rasp takes it as a professional compliment.
- Tallow: Let him hold a smoker once. He held it like a baby bird. She liked him after that.
- Hessa: Hessa asked what is in the clay. Rasp told her, in detail. Hessa stood further off, and Rasp respects her for it.
- Tebbet: Two who would rather do it from a distance. They compare ranges at night, as other soldiers compare boots.
- Darrut: Darrut wants to know if a cusser could take down Aren's north gate. Rasp has worked it out. She will not tell him.
- Orvas: Orvas keeps the horse on the far side of her. She thinks that is wise, and says so.
- Mirrel: Mirrel keeps a separate salve for her hands, and does not lecture. Rasp counts that a kindness.
- Yorin: She asked him how close lightning has to be to light a fuse. He walks on the other side of the column now.
