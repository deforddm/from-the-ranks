## Two squads, one save

- **Threads:** `S.thread` is `'t'` (the Third, lent to Keneb's column) or `'l'` (the Letherii, the Sixth Company). `B7_CH = {1:'t', 2:'l', 3:'t', 4:'l', 5:'t', 6:'l', 7:'t'}`; `startChapter(n)` calls `b7Use(B7_CH[n])` first.
- **What each squad keeps:** `B7_KEYS` = Book Six's list less `fever`, plus `debt`, `debt0`. The waiting squad lives in `S.th[other]`. Shared: `S.f`, `S.stats`, `S.chapter`, `S.chapters`, `S.diff`, and `S.b7` (the Rising and the file).
- **`b7Use(t)`** swaps, sets `TPL.sgt` from `BOOKSNAP.hoc.sgt` (the Third) or `BOOKSNAP.mt.sgt` (the Letherii), clears the portrait cache; idempotent. `setBook('rg')` re-applies the sergeant.

- **Helpers:** `b7On()`, `b7Thread()`, `b7Other()`, `b7L()`, `b7Third()`, `b7Unit(t)`, `b7In(id)`, `b7OtherName()`, `b7Coin(n)` (docks for the Letherii, silver for the Third), `b7SgtLook()`, `b7ChCount()`. `B7_TH[t]`: `book, look, unit, squad, army, who, core, fresh, recruits, lvl, cap`.

## The Rising (plan question 4; rg-canon §8)

- **One number, 0–10, `S.b7.rise`, shared by both squads, starting at 2** (canon: the Letherii will not rise, W14, and fight beside the Edur, W17: so it is earned). `S.b7.hi` remembers its height.
- `b7Rise(n, why)`: moves it (n may be negative), clamps, notes (with the tier's words when it crosses one), tallies `b7Rose` / `b7Fell`, updates the HUD, saves. Returns the new value.
- `b7Rising()`, `b7RiseTier()` (`quiet` 0–2, `stirring` 3–5, `rising` 6–8, `risen` 9–10), `b7Open()` (≥3), `b7Guides()` (≥6). `B7_RISEW` holds the tiers' words.
- **The Third's face:** a check with `rise:true` gets the named edge **"The Rising"**: +1 stirring, +2 rising, +3 risen (an `edgesFor` wrap).
- **The Letherii's face: the file.** Modelled as a **second counter plus a derived threshold** (Claude's call): `S.b7.file`, moved by `b7Filed(n, why)` (tallied `b7Filed`), and the heat `b7Heat()` = file + floor(Rising / 3). `b7HeatTier()`: `clean` 0–2, `noted` 3–4, `watched` 5–6, `wanted` 7+ (`B7_HEATW`). A `rise:true` check gets **"Your file at the Patriotists"**: −1 watched, −2 wanted. `b7Wanted()` is the writer's arrest branch. So the Letherii's own acts and the Third's success both raise their danger, which is the plan's "one system with two faces".
- **Widgets:** `risingHTML()` for a node's `html` (the Rising for both; the file for the Letherii). The HUD pill (`updChain`) shows "Rising N", and "File <tier>" once the Letherii's is past `clean`.
- The journal opens with the Rising (and the file for the Letherii) and the other squad's level; the count shows the Rising now and at its height, and pages added to the file; Deeds has a "The Rising" section.
