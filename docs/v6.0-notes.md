## Starting a game

- `newStateMOI(name, importId?, sid?)` → a migrated Book Three save: `book:'moi'`, squad `MOI_PORDER = ['sgt','brisk','kettle','tuft','ohl']`, `ally:{trust:5, dread:1}`, silver 15, `inv {sharper:2, burner:1, cusser:1, salve:2, smoker:1}`, level 1, `chapter:0`. The title's **Begin** does `S = newStateMOI(name, importId); S.diff = diff; save(); startChapter(1)` (no prologue, no intro screen). With no Book Three chapter registered, Begin says "Book Three is still being written" and stays on the title.
- **The import** (title field "Bring a Book One sergeant and the Fourth", shown when a finished Book One save has `chapters[7] === 'outlaw'`): picking one fills and locks the name field (a fresh start unlocks it) and the new save gets its own slot (the Book One save is never overwritten).
  - `S.squad`: the sergeant plus every Book One squadmate in that save's squad who is alive and followed (`!(o.dead||{})[k] && (o.f.c7_follow||{})[k] !== false`), from `brisk, kettle, tuft, ohl, ellis, vell`, in that order. `S.loy`: theirs, clamped to −2..3. `S.f.sgtScar` comes across.
  - `S.imported = {from, sid, road:'outlaw', lvl, dead:[ids], f:{…}}`; `f` keeps `c7_tav, c7_letter, c7_debt, c7_chub, c6_tuft, c7_tuftCut, c5_tuftMarked, c4_key, c4_vell, c6_key, c7_wjKnows, c7_wjReport, sgtScar, c2_ellisJoined, c5_ellisThrough, c7_ellisBack, c5_cusserUsed` (when set) and `list` (Book One's `listCount()`). Level is kept in `S.imported.lvl` but the game starts at level 1.
  - `b3Was(k)` reads `S.imported.f[k]` (undefined on a fresh start). `b3Fallen()` lists the dead ids, `b3FallenNames()` says them ("Tuft and Vell"). The Book One dead are **not** in `S.dead` (that is Book Three's own dead); mourn them by name where it fits.
  - Ellis and Vell keep their Book One templates; their sheet quest moves on (Book Three text). Ellis's hunt for Toc never succeeds (series rule 5).
- **Fresh start** text may assume only: all five came out of Book One; Tuft knows the child is Tattersail; Brisk's Tav is alive in the Host; they are on the outlaw road (the narrator never vouches for the outlawing: rule 1).

## Systems (all guarded by `S.book === 'moi'`)

1. **The Alliance**: `S.ally = {trust, dread}`, both 0..12 (`ALLY_MAX`).
   - **`ally(dTrust, dDread, why)`** changes them, notes it ("Parley: trust +1, dread +1."), tallies (`trustUp`, `trustDown`, `dreadUp`) and updates the HUD pill (`#chainp`: "Trust n · Dread n").
   - **`allyHTML(road)`**: the widget for a node's `html` (two bars and a caption; styled like `chainHTML`).
   - `b3Trust()`, `b3Dread()`, **`b3Mind()`** → `'haunted'` (dread ≥ 10), `'shaken'` (≥ 6) or `''`, for text.
   - **Allies**: a fight may name `aid:{kind, at:[x,y], need:8}` (or an array). At trust ≥ `need` (default 8) the ally fights beside the squad, scaled by level (`mkAlly`), with a log line; below it, a line says who did not come. Allied foes: `b3_rhivi_rider`, `b3_barghast`, `b3_andii`, `b3_greysword`, `b3_moranth`. A stage two inherits the fight's `aid` unless it names its own.
   - **Shaken** (dread ≥ 6 when the fight starts): the squad −1 to hit for rounds 1–2. **Haunted** (≥ 10): −1 to hit all fight and −1 initiative. Log lines say so.
   - **`b3Rest(t)`**: a full rest (free in Book Three: the Host feeds its own) that also lowers dread by 2. Charge for a rest in your node if the place says otherwise.
2. **Poisoned warrens**: set **`S.f.b3_domin = 1`** when the squad is in the Domin (from Chapter 5; Capustan's siege ground from Chapter 3). Then a squadmate's strain-costing spell may **burn** its caster for `1d4 + max(0, chapter − 3)` (never below 1 health): 10% Ch3, 15% Ch4, 20% Ch5, 25% Ch6, 30% Ch7 (`b3BurnPct()`). Spirit-craft (`a.spirit`) and Elder tricks (`a.elder`) never burn; nor does anything under the Leper. **`S.f.b3_burns`** counts them. Give a trick `spirit:true` if it is Rhivi or Barghast craft.
3. **The squad away for one fight**: `def.away = ['kettle']` (or `stage2.away`): fought without them; they stay in the squad.
4. **`def.hurtMost`**: after the fight **`S.f.b3_hurtMost`** = the squadmate worst hurt for their size (never the sergeant, never a recruit).
5. **`def.lastStand`**: a hold-out; won or lost it goes on to `def.after` with the experience, no god, no retry. **`S.f.b3_lastHeld`** = 1 held / 0 went down; **`S.f.b3_lastRounds`**. Every Book Three fight also writes **`S.f.b3_lastRoundsFought`**.
6. **Mortal fights** (`mortal:true`, `kill(id)`, `S.dead`) work in Book Three (tested).
7. **Enemies** for chapters to place (sprites by the artist): **`b3_beklite`**, **`b3_seerdomin`**, **`b3_tenescowri`**, **`b3_khell`** (a K'ell Hunter, boss, 60 health). Do not re-register these ids in a chapter's `foes` (the lint fails an id clash); add your own `b3_` foes beside them, with `kind:` pointing at a sprite.
