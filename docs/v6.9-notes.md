# From the Ranks v6.9.0: the gods' boons

Sun Oct 18, 2026. Open question Q1 (asked Oct 8). David's answers, Oct 18: restart the fight with the boons (not resume); two boons at most on Story; Bridgeburner gets them; the table as drafted.

## The rule

When the whole squad goes down, the most loyal squadmate's god answers (as since v3.12) and the fight restarts from its snapshot. New: the god leaves a **boon** on the fight. Boons are keyed by **god**, so the same god gives the same boon in every book. They stack when more than one god answers the same fight (one per god), last the fight and its second ground, and end when the fight is won. On Story, where the gods always answer, the two newest hold.

| God | Key | Boon |
|---|---|---|
| Hood | `hood` | the first time each squadmate would go down, they stay up at 1 (`S.boons.spared[id]`) |
| Oponn | `oponn` | +2 to hit; the first attack each round is rolled twice (`u.pullRound`) |
| Shadowthrone | `shadow` | enemies −2 to hit the squad; nobody on the squad's side can be flanked |
| Soliel | `soliel` | each squadmate heals 3 at the start of their turn, bleeding stops |
| Cotillion | `rope` | +2 move; the squad's moves draw no free swings (`provokes`, `leaveCost`) |
| Fener | `boar` | +3 damage on every squad hit |
| Treach | `tiger` | +2 damage; +2 to hit on an enemy a squadmate already stands beside |
| the spirits (Sathi) | `cold` | an enemy that starts its turn next to a squadmate: `u.mv` halved for the turn (`u.mvFull` restores it next turn), −1 to hit (`u.coldTurn`) |
| the Queen of Dreams | `dream` | enemies −2 to hit; the Semk bolt always at half; a daze is always a stagger (47) |
| the grandmother | `ground` | the Book Two sputter never fires; each squadmate heals 2 at the start of their turn |
| the Rhivi spirits | `herd` | +2 move; enemy ranged attacks −3 |
| Togg and Fanderay | `wolves` | +1 armour; an enemy that starts its turn next to a squadmate takes 1d4 cold |

## Code

- `src/31c_stakes.js`: `BOONS` (by god name as `PATRONS[id].god`), `BOON_BY_KEY`, `boonsOn()`, `boon(k)`, `patronBoon(who)`, `boonGrant(fight, b)`, `boonsClear()`, `boonTurnStart(u)` (called first in `squadTurnStart`), `boonFoeTurnStart(u)` (called in `nextTurn` for every unit), `boonsHTML()`, `boonsLine()`.
- `src/31_battle.js`: `hurt` (Hood), `mkParty` (move, armour), `boonHit` + `atkCalc` (to-hit and the flank), `attack` (the pull, the damage), `provokes` and `leaveCost` (the Rope), `startBattle` (the chips `#boons` between the map and the unit bar, the log line), `win` (clear), `lose` (the boon named on the god's page; `boonGrant` on Rise, after the snapshot restore so a second god joins the first).
- `src/47_book2.js`: the sputter wrapper honours `ground`; the Semk bolt and the daze honour `dream`.
- `src/37_chapters.js`: `S.boons = null` at a chapter start; `chapterAgain` clears it too.
- `src/01_style.css`: `.boons`, `.boon`, `.note.boonnote`.
- State: `S.boons = {fight, gods:[keys newest last], spared:{id:1}}`. `boonsOn()` matches `fight` against `B.id` (or `S.battle` before `B` exists, for `mkParty`). The fight's snapshot carries it, so a reload mid-fight and a second ground keep it.
- Test: `tools/boons_test.mjs` (24 checks): every patron has a boon; the sergeant's god follows the book; the god's page; Rise grants, chips and log; each mechanic; the second ground; the win clears; the Story cap; a phone fits six chips.

## Not done, on purpose

- The balance bot (`tools/balance.mjs`) measures a fight's first attempt; boons only apply after a loss, so its numbers don't move. Q2 (Bridgeburner's early fights) stays open and is the right place to look at them together.
- Sigil art exists for Book One's six gods only (`patrons/<id>`); the chips are text, so every book's gods show the same way.
