## Witness (plan, settled 2: the main track)

**What it is.** What each squad still believes it marches for, 0 to 10, in that squad's own half (`S.b10w = {n, lo, hi}`): two squads, two answers. Book Nine's light beats become the book's system, the way Rations was Book Nine's.

**Tiers** (`b10WitTier(n)`, words `B10_WITW`):

| n | tier | words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | `hollow` | nothing left to march for |
| 2–3 | `doubt` | marching because the column does |
| 4–6 | `holding` | still marching for something |
| 7–8 | `kept` | marching for each other |
| 9–10 | `witnessed` | their own witness |

**Where it starts.** Fresh: 5 (`holding`). From Book Nine: 5 + kept − doubt of that squad's Book Nine beats (`o.b9.wit`; the Third's are c3, c5, c7, the Letherii's c4, c6), held to 2..8. So a Third that kept all three starts at 8, one that doubted all three at 2.

**What moves it** (`b10Witness(n, why)`: noted with the reason and any tier change, clamped, tallied `b10WitUp` / `b10WitDown`, HUD, saved):
- **The beat**, one in every chapter (`b10Beat('kept' | 'doubt', why)`, `B10BEAT` 1–7): kept +1, doubt −1; the first answer stands; recorded in `S.b10.beat.c<n>`; tallied `b10Kept` / `b10Doubt`.
- **The key**: each chapter's key may move it, as the brief's keys table says (most ±1, Chapter 5's `give` +2).
- **The wear** (the engine's own move): nobody tells the army why. At the start of Chapters 3, 4 and 5 the playing squad loses 1, once (`B10_WEAR`, flags `b10_wear3..5`).
- **Two fixed anchors** (the brief): the lake in Ch 5, +1 ("water under the column"); the Fourth over the ridge in Ch 7, +1 ("somebody came").
- **A writer's own scene**: at most ±1 a chapter, plus the one "yes, but" that may cost 1 Witness (the brief's rule).

**What it does.**
- **Checks:** a check with `witness:true` gets the edge **"Witness"**: witnessed +2, kept +1, holding 0, doubt −1, hollow −2. Any other check, while `hollow`, gets **"Nothing to march for"** −1.
- **Fights:** `B10_WITFIGHT` is a to-hit modifier for every squadmate by tier: **hollow −1, witnessed +1**, nothing else (David, Nov 10, plan decision 8; this line corrected by the continuity read). With a zero the fight log says nothing.
- **Roads (the endings):** `b10RoadOpen(r, t)`: the `kolanse` ending (stay and help the drought country) needs `holding` or better (`B10_ROADNEED`). Chapters 6 and 7 show it greyed with a line when closed. The ending pages read the tier for their words.
- **Words:** the sergeants' quest lines, the journal's first line, and the coda's page for each Book Ten squad ("At the end, …").

**How it shows** (as Rations did): the HUD pill **"Witness N"** (red at 3 or less) in Rations' place; `witnessHTML(t?)` for a node (a bar and the tier's words); the journal's first line; the count (now, lowest, beats kept, beats doubted, thirst); Deeds' "Witness" section (lowest and highest across every sergeant, beats kept, came through to the end).

**Rations are not carried** (plan, Claude's call): `b10RationLine(t)` turns a transfer's Book Nine Rations into one line for that squad's first chapter (Ch 1 and Ch 2's intros); empty on a fresh start.
