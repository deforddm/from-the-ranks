## What a writer needs to know

- A scene names its place with `scene:'b3_c3_walls_night'` (the backdrop) and may name an ambience with `amb:'siege_night'`. Either works: every scene, decor and battle `style` in the source resolves through the engine's `PLACE` table to one of 40 ambiences, and `tools/audio_test.mjs` fails if a new name doesn't. **When you add a scene, decor or battle style, add its row to `PLACE`** (or make sure the prefix default is right: `b2_*` falls to the desert, `b3_*` to the northern plain, `*_night` to the night version). Battles also use `dark:true` for the night version.
- The ambience names: `wind camp camp_night interior inn plain dusk night hills city city_night tunnel dark storm fete lake` (Book One), `desert desert_dusk desert_night column column_night oasis forest forest_night river river_night sea sea_night siege siege_night burning` (Book Two), `snow snow_night burnt burnt_night herds rain barrow coral spawn` (Book Three). New books add their own under their own prefix.
- Sounds, by name (`AUDIO.play(name)`): UI `click open close save pick trick up coin dice flip shuffle reveal`; fights `step sword blunt spear claw sling bow miss hurt death growl snarl bark yelp whine howl`; munitions `sharper burner cusser smoker boom slam`; warrens `magic` (Mockra's shimmer) `shadow heal fire ice grey air`; Book Two's `pour stone rumble chomp`; music `win lose chapter house(houseName) choice`. A landed blow picks its own sound from the attacker's verb (`AUDIO.blow(verb, ranged)`): mace, cudgel, mallet, fist and mattock are blunt; spear, lance, pike, halberd, knife and flint are a point; claws, teeth and "reaches for" are claws; sling; bows and quarrels; grey sorcery and ice. A new foe only needs a verb.
- Stingers fire themselves: `chapter` from `showChapterIntro`, `house` when the turned card is read, `choice` when a choice sets the chapter's key flag (`c3_key`, `b2_c3_key`, `b3_c3_key`: `keyWatch` in `src/16_skill_checks.js`). A new book's key flags must follow the pattern or add their prefix to `keyFlag()`.

## The voices

| | Book One `gotm` | Book Two `dg` | Book Three `moi` |
|---|---|---|---|
| scale, root | Phrygian on D2 | Phrygian dominant (hijaz) on E2 | Aeolian on C2 |
| pad | detuned saws | saw and triangle, wider detune | saws through two formants (an open "ah") |
| lines | flute, cello, glass | reed (grace note, bend), oud (pluck, a thumb note under) | choir, bells, cello |
| battle lead | horn | reed, an octave up | low horn, a sung note under it |
| drums | kick and tom | frame drum: dum and tak | war drum and a rim rattle |
| bell | yes | a finger cymbal instead | yes, louder |
| tempos | 54 / 60 / 50 / 52 / 96 / 84 | 58 / 64 / 52 / 54 / 104 / 88 | 50 / 56 / 48 / 50 / 88 / 80 |
| reverb send | .3 | .2 | .45 |

(Tempos: title / calm / end / dread / battle / dark.) The voice is `S.book`, or the title's `BOOK`; `setBook` calls `AUDIO.revoice()` so the title's music changes with the cover shown. The twelve motifs and the chord progressions are scale degrees, so they work in every voice; a voice may add its own drum patterns and ostinato (`pats`, `fills`, `ost`).

## Reverb

Two convolvers, cross-faded by `setSpace`: a 2.6 s stone hall and a 0.9 s open air. Tunnels, cellars, interiors, inns, barrows, Coral, the Spawn and burning Hissar are the hall; everything else is outdoors. `SPACE` in the engine names the exceptions.

## Tests

`tools/audio_test.mjs` (157 checks, about two minutes): renders every sound effect, every music cue in every voice (10 s) and every ambience (4 s) in an `OfflineAudioContext` at 24 kHz with seeded dice, and checks each makes a sound, stays under the limiter (peak ≤ 1) and carries no NaN; the music and the beds must still be sounding in the last 40%. Then it reads the source for every `scene:`, `decor:`, `style:` and `amb:` and asserts each resolves to a real ambience, that the voice follows the save's book, and that the verb table names blows as expected. Two faults it caught while being written: the volume ramp on a fresh context swallowed the first 60 ms (now the offline path sets gains directly), and the stinger layers faded from zero to zero (now `sting(life)` holds them at full and fades at the end).
