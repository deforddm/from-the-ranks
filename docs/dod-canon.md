## Plan corrections (the gate: does a planned beat contradict canon?)

**Yes, one (1); the rest are small.** Each fix has a recommendation first.

### 1. The Letherii are not apart at the end: they are in the battle (Ch 6)

- **Canon:** the Nah'ruk fall on "the Bonehunters and their Letherii escort" (WC23). W *K'Chain Nah'ruk*: they attacked "the Bonehunters and the Letherii Army". After the battle Sinter credits "Ruthan Gudd, Quick Ben, Fiddler, the Khundryl, and the Letherii" (RT4 S6). Brys "survived the battle with the K'Chain Nah'ruk" (W *Brys Beddict*) [H, W + R].
- Tavore accepts the Letherii as an **escort** before the march (R5 S3, S10) [1].
- **The real parting is in TCG:** the Bonehunters cross the Glass Desert, and "the Perish, Letherii, and Bolkando marched south along the coast" (W *Tavore Paran*, TCG) [1]. Book Nine must not close it (rule 85).
- **What the Letherii did in the battle: not found** [O]. Which brigades, where they stood, and Brys's part are §10 Q2.
- **The plan's Ch 6** ("the Letherii and the Bonehunters apart near the end") can't stand as written.
- **Fix (A), recommended: Ch 6 is the battle's day, from the escort.** The dust on the horizon, the escort's line, the Letherii's own stand on a stretch no source names. It is not the mortal fight. The roads are chosen at dusk, when the Malazans' rearguard is still fighting. The Third's Ch 7 is the same battle's other end and its dark. The two squads never meet: different ground, as in Book Seven.
- **Fix (B):** Ch 6 is a water-and-forage detail sent off from the escort's column. It sees the dust from far off and gets back as the fight ends. Truer to the plan's "apart", but it keeps the Letherii out of the book's climax.

### 2. The thirst is TCG's; DoD's want is fever, thin forage and hoarding (Ch 3, 5)

- **The killing thirst is in TCG:** in the Glass Desert "after water ran out, the soldiers were forced to drink blood and urine"; Tavore ends it with Bugg's dagger and a lake (W *Tavore Paran*, TCG). Blistig hoards water barrels and stabs Pores for them (W *Blistig*, TCG ch 11, ch 20) [1].
- **What DoD has** (the Rations anchors, §2):
  - fever among the Malazans as they leave Letheras, and "Brys's water cure has worked" (R11 S13) [1];
  - the Wastes "emptied" of their great beasts (Cafal, R10 S7), though bhederin still walk them (R10 S1) [1];
  - Pores hoarding, and his wagon ransacked (R22 S11, S14) [1];
  - Moranth munitions running low, so Hedge has Bavedict make new ones (W *Hedge*) [1];
  - a ten-year drought in Kolanse (R5) [1];
  - the Letherii's own supply train (T p. 763, via the plan).
- **Fix:** Rations bites as short rations, fever, foul water and hoarding. Never death by thirst, never drinking blood, never water barrels hoarded by a Fist (rule 85). Ch 5's "the army's thirst" becomes "short rations and the hoard".

### 3. Day or night (Ch 7)

- The Nah'ruk are first seen as "what looks like a dust-storm" (WC23), so they come by **day** [1].
- No source loaded says the fight runs into night [O]. ME: the marines and heavies "cover the army's retreat" [F].
- **Fix, recommended:** Ch 7 starts late in the day. The Third's mortal stand is part of the rearguard as the light goes, and "the Nah'ruk night" is what comes after dark. Page check §10 Q1. If the page shows a fight that ends in daylight, the chapter becomes "the Nah'ruk day" with no other change.

### 4. Who is not on the field (Ch 5, 7)

- **Gesler and Stormy** are taken from their tent by a Shi'gal Assassin before the battle (R22 S5, S16). They come back with the K'Chain Che'Malle in the second battle (R24.1 S3–S4, S10–S20) [1].

### 5. Tavore's speeches (Witness beats)

### 6. The Malazan drill (Ch 2)

- **Canon's drillers** are Corporal Tarr and Cuttle, with Cuttle playing the "Braven role". They needle the Letherii about their losses, march them to exhaustion, and Fiddler gives the wrecked Letherii practical advice (R5 S1, S3, S8) [1].
- **Fix:** the Letherii squad's company is drilled by an unnamed Malazan sergeant of the heavies. Never Tarr's or Cuttle's company, and Fiddler never stops by.

### 7. Hedge's Letherii company (Ch 2, 4)

- Hedge takes in a company of Letherii recruits and names them **Bridgeburners**: Sunrise, with Sweetlard and Rumjugs as its sergeants. Bavedict, a Letherii alchemist, makes munitions he calls "kittens" (R11 S14, S16; W *Hedge*; RT4 S5; TCG Extended DP) [H].
- **Fix:** the Letherii squad never joins Hedge's company and is never called Bridgeburners (rule 12 and rule 93). A rumour line at most: "a Malazan ghost is recruiting".

### 8. The Reading (Ch 1)

- Before the march Fiddler deals a Reading of the Deck in Letheras. It overflows into the city: dead animals stir, two dragons burst out of an inn, and the old gods guarding Seren's child catch fire. The Errant gatecrashes it, and then attacks Brys, until Ublala Pung knocks him down (WC1, WC3) [1].
- Nearly every soldier has left the barracks ahead of it (WC3) [1].
- **Fix, recommended:** Ch 1 sits beside it. The Third is out in the city that night, sees a dead thing get up, and sees a roof go in far off. Never the Reading's room, the inn, the Errant or Brys.
