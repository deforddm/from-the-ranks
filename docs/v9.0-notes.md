## Two squads, one save

- **Threads:**
  - `S.thread` is `'r'`: the Third of Blistig's 9th in the Fourteenth (Book Two's and Four's squad).
  - Or `'h'`: the Fourth of Onearm's Host (Books One and Three).
- **Which squad plays:** `B6_CH = {1:'r', 2:'h', 3:'r', 4:'h', 5:'r', 6:'h', 7:'r'}`. `startChapter(n)` calls `b6Use(B6_CH[n])` first.
- **What each squad keeps:** `B6_KEYS`: `name, squad, camp, loy, gear, picks, kit, tricks, inv, lvl, xp, dead, parked, wounds, silver, picksDue, fever, imported, trail`.
  - The squad playing has these on `S` as in every book.
  - The squad waiting has them in `S.th[other]`.
  - Shared between the two: `S.f` (the book's flags), `S.stats`, `S.chapter`, `S.chapters`, `S.diff`.
- **`b6Use(t)`:**
  - Swaps the squads and sets `TPL.sgt` from `BOOKSNAP.hoc.sgt` (`'r'`) or `BOOKSNAP.moi.sgt` (`'h'`).
  - Clears the portrait cache.
  - Idempotent.
  - `setBook('bh')` re-applies the sergeant for `S.thread`.
- **Helpers:**
  - `b6On()`, `b6Thread()`, `b6Other()`, `b6Unit(t)` ("the Third" / "the Fourth"), `b6In(id)`, `b6OtherName()`.
  - `B6_TH[t]`: the squad's `book, unit, squad, army, core, fresh, recruits`.
- **The sergeants' names:** `S.name` is the name of the sergeant playing.
  - Claude's call: the roster line on the title shows whichever squad played last. The new-sergeant screen (part 2) asks for both names.

## Starting a game

- **`newStateBH(nameR, nameH, impR, impH, sid?)`:**
  - Each squad starts at **level 12**, or its transfer's level if higher; the cap is **18**.
  - Each squad starts with 20 silver and the Fourteenth's issue (2 sharpers, 1 burner, 1 cusser, 1 smoker, 3 salves).
- **Fresh defaults:**
  - The Third: Haddat, Tangle, Marrow, Mirrel. Sathi fell at the Barrow, as Book Four's fresh start has it.
  - The Fourth: Brisk, Kettle, Tuft, Ohl.
- **Transfers** (plan question 2):
  - `b6QualR(o)`: a finished Book Four save on the `line` road.
  - `b6QualH(o)`: a finished Book Three save on the `host` road.
  - `b6Carry(o)` brings every living soldier on the roster (five walk, the rest wait at camp), plus worn gear and unworn kit, talents and veteran picks, loyalty (−2 to 3), the tricks of those who come, munitions and salves (at least 2). The dead stay dead.
  - `b6ImpR(id)`, `b6ImpH(id)` and `b6ImpList(t)` (from the title roster) feed the new-sergeant screen.

## Fever (plan question 4; bh-canon §8)

- **`b6Catch(id, why)`:** the soldier has the fever, noted, tallied `b6Caught`.
  - Ressa never catches it: she had it and lived (her bio).
  - The dead and those already fevered are left alone.
- **`b6Cure(id, how)`:** breaks it, noted, tallied `b6Cured`. `b6CanCure()`: a healer walks (`HEALER()`), or after Chapter 4 `S.f.b6_soliel` (Soliel heals through the carriers, bh-canon §8).
- **The cost:** in a fight a fevered soldier has −4 maximum health and −1 to hit (`mkParty`, `u.fevered`).
- **The list:** `b6Fevered(id)`, `b6FeverList()`. `feverHTML()` gives a node's `html` box.
- **Fights that spread it:** a fight def with `feverRisk:'<why>'` gives the fever to one walking squadmate when it ends, win or lose. Writers mark the plague villages, the cultists and the G'danisban streets.
- **Tone:** never lurid. The words are the soldier's, and short.
