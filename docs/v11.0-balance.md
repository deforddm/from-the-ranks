# v11.0 balance: every fight in Book Eight (*Time Served*), tuned per fight

**David's targets** (Fri Nov 6, 2026; confirmed for Book Eight Mon Nov 9, 2026, about 7:41 AM PST: "tune to the series targets, Book Eight tables only"), measured as the smart bot's first-try win %, per fight:
- **Soldier** about 90% (accept 85–97%). Kept near 100%: the book's first fight (`b8_c1_alley`) and the survive fights (`b8_c4_edge`, survive 5; `b8_c6_street`, survive 4).
- **Bridgeburner** about 75–80% (accept 65–85%), and no fight under 60%.
- **The mortal fight** `b8_c6_knives`: on Bridgeburner it should cost a life now and then, inside the same band; on Soldier, a normal ~90% fight.
- Soldier never harder than Bridgeburner on the same fight, beyond the run-to-run noise.

**Where the numbers live** (src/140_book8.js only; no earlier book, no engine file and no chapter file was touched):
- `B8SOLDIER` — Soldier only, merged into `SOLDIER_FIGHT` by fight id. 21 rows; the first fight and the two survive fights have none.
- `B8GAP` — Bridgeburner only, 24 rows, one a fight (`{ac +, atk +, hp ×, dmg +}`, as `B7GAP`), applied in Book Eight's own `mkFoe` wrapper (the Hound is never boosted).
- `B8GAP_ROW` — `{ac:5, atk:5, hp:3.5, dmg:7}`, for a `b8_` fight added later without its own row (it was `null`).

**The Contract** (`tools/balance.mjs`, `setupTTH`): every chapter is fought with `S.b8 = {con:4, hi:4, lo:3}`, the brief's middle road. So `b8Roofs` puts three knives on Ch 5's road (`b8_c5_knives`, plus its crossbow), four knives in the Ch 6 mortal fight (`b8_c6_knives`: its own knife plus three), and two knives on Ch 7's roofs (`b8_c7_roofs`: one fewer than Ch 6, because the harness does not set `b8_c6_knivesDown`). Contract 4 also gives every `contract:true` check its −1. A player at Contract 6+ meets veterans (and at 10 the Grand Master's knife) on those tiles under the same row, so those three fights are harder than measured here; at 0–1 they are easier (Ch 7's roofs empty). The rows were not scaled by the Contract: that would be an engine change.

**How it was tuned**: tools/balance.mjs, smart bot, `--noellis=0 --naive=0`, each candidate injected by `--patch` (no rebuild), then the final tables built into src and measured unpatched. The untuned book won every fight 100% on both difficulties with 1–12% health lost: the Fourth at 16–19 against street knives, cultists and bravos. So the ladder from v10.2 (5% health a step) was far too fine; Book Eight used a coarser one, level q: armour +⌊q/2⌋, to hit +⌈q/2⌉, health ×(1 + q/4), damage +⌈q/2⌉ + 2 (q 10 = `{ac:5, atk:5, hp:3.5, dmg:7}`). Two calibration probes on every fight (q 10 and q 14, 6 runs each) seeded each fight's level on each side; an automatic tuner (scratchpad `tune8/auto8.py`, adapted from v10.2's `tune/auto.py`) bisected each fight at 10 runs, then confirmed at 20 (pooled at the same level); a handful of fights on a cliff between two levels got a hand row in between (`b8_c2_raid` and `b8_c6_wall` on Bridgeburner, `b8_c3_road`, `b8_c6_wall`, `b8_c6_knives`, `b8_c7_roofs` on Soldier). Twenty runs swing about ±10, so a fight at 85% here may be 75–95%. The machine was shared with another agent's work all session (load 10 on 2 CPUs), which slows the harness but changes no result.

**The mortal fight** (`b8_c6_knives`, Contract 4: four knives). Bridgeburner final row `{ac:4, atk:4, hp:3.0, dmg:6}`: 18 of 20 won (90%), 1.7 dead a fight counting the two losses (a loss kills everyone down, the sergeant aside); in the 18 wins, 13 cost at least one squadmate (0–3, mean 1.3). One step harder (`{ac:4, atk:5, hp:3.25, dmg:7}`) was 70–80% won with 2.6 dead a fight, which is a slaughter rather than "now and then", so the easier step was kept. Soldier `{ac:6, atk:6, hp:4.2, dmg:8}`: 17 of 20 won (85%), and the bot still loses one or two in most wins. That is the bot's way with a mortal fight (it fights through downs that a player would pull back from), so real deaths should run lower than this.

## Outside the band, and why

- **Soldier near 100%** (above 97%) in seven fights besides the three kept there by rule: `b8_c1_chancers`, `b8_c3_road`, `b8_c3_ridge`, `b8_c4_drunk`, `b8_c5_grass`, `b8_c5_knives`, `b8_c7_roofs`. Each is won every time with 18–43% health lost, and the next step up was a cliff: `b8_c3_road` went 100% → 70% for one ladder step, `b8_c5_knives` and `b8_c6_knives` 100% → 65–75%. `b8_c5_knives` was also held down by the Soldier-under-Bridgeburner cap in tools/fightrow_test.mjs (its first row measured 90% but its foe came out 1.11× Bridgeburner's; health 4.25 → 4.0). `b8_c7_roofs` at Contract 4 is two knives on a roof: it needs to-hit +12 to bite at all, and its Soldier row was kept lighter than Bridgeburner's on purpose, since a high Contract puts veterans under the same row.
- **Bridgeburner over 85%**: `b8_c1_den`, `b8_c1_hunt`, `b8_c6_wall`, `b8_c6_knives` (90%), `b8_c5_grass`, `b8_c7_doorway` (95%), and the survive fight `b8_c4_edge` (100%). The tuner measured the same rows at 75%, 80%, 80%, 80% and 75% in earlier 20-run passes (`den`, `hunt`, `wall`, `grass`, `doorway`), so pooled over 40 runs each sits at 82–87%: the noise of 20 runs, not a miss. `b8_c6_knives` was left on the easier step on purpose (above). `b8_c4_edge` measured 85% at the same row in the confirm pass: five rounds of pilgrims with waves, and the bot holds a survive fight well (as Books One, Two, Four and Five's survive fights).
- **Soldier a little under Bridgeburner** (5 points, inside the noise): `b8_c1_hunt` 85 / 90, `b8_c6_knives` 85 / 90, `b8_c7_doorway` 90 / 95. In every fight the Soldier row's foe is at most 1.1× Bridgeburner's by tools/fightrow_test.mjs's threat measure (Bridgeburner's blow ×1.2).
- **No fight under 60%** on either difficulty; the lowest is `b8_c3_ridge` on Bridgeburner at 65%.
