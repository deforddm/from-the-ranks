# From the Ranks: meeting the canon (the new line, David, Nov 15)

David, Nov 15, 12:06 AM PST: "It has always been my intention for the squads to closely interact and intersect with canon characters, I just don't ever want us to control one of them." The rules are in `series-canon.md` §(a0). In short:
- **Locked:** canon outcomes and deaths; who is where, when; no early spoilers; no canon viewpoint (visions excepted, as direct canon reads).
- **Lifted:** the "never meets / never speaks with / at a distance" rules, the never-use names, and the Burn's Sleep year ban.

The chapter tests already allow it (v13.7). What follows is a short list of meetings worth adding. Each one:
- puts a canon character where the books put them;
- gives the squad a scene with them (a talk, an order, a shared job, a fight beside them);
- changes nothing canon decides.

**Recommended first** is marked ★: the meetings the books most obviously set up, or that pay off a thread the game already has. Each is a talk node or two, with a small reward where it fits (a trick, an item, a loyalty beat), so each book can take them as one minor version.

## Book One (*Gardens of the Moon*): the Fourth
Book One already meets the most canon (Whiskeyjack, Paran, Quick Ben, Kalam, Fiddler, Hedge, Kruppe, Crokus, Rallick, Lorn, Tool).
- ★ **Fiddler and Hedge under the crossing (Ch 3–4).** The two sappers mining the intersection. Kettle works beside them for a night. The mines stay unfired (rule 10). This sets up Book Ten's Hedge line.
- **Tattersail before Pale falls (Prologue/Ch 1).** One talk in the cadre tent, before her death and rebirth. Rule 4 holds: nothing of the child.

## Book Two (*Deadhouse Gates*): the Third on the Chain
Book Two already meets Duiker, Coltaine, Bult, Lull, List and Sormo.
- ★ **Coltaine's order at the Sekala crossing (Ch 3).** Coltaine gives the Third its job himself, in one line. His death at Aren is untouched.
- **Nil and Nether (Ch 4–5).** The warlock children ask the squad's healer about the warrens going wrong.

## Book Three (*Memories of Ice*): the Fourth with the Host
- ★ **Whiskeyjack at the parley (Ch 1).** He knows the Fourth from Book One ("All five"). He asks after the leg, and Brisk tells him to see Mallet. His death at Coral stays as canon has it.
- ★ **Hedge before Coral (Ch 7).** The last time the Fourth sees him alive. Nothing hints at his death; Book Ten pays it off.
- **Quick Ben at the Barghast camp (Ch 5).** One exchange about the White Face clans.

## Book Four (*House of Chains*): the Third in the Fourteenth
- ★ **Fiddler's marines on the march (Ch 3–4).** The Third shares a fire with Fiddler (still called Strings) and Gesler. Rule 12's first half holds: the Third is not marines, and is told so.
- **Keneb as captain (Ch 1–2).** He inspects the squad at the muster. He becomes Fist after Raraku, as canon has it.
- **Temul (Ch 2).** The boy-commander of the Wickan youths, met at the trees.

## Book Five (*Midnight Tides*): the Letherii, years before *Gardens*
Lock 2 binds hard here: no Malazans, nothing after the conquest.
- ★ **Brys Beddict, King's Champion (Ch 1, Letheras).** He watches the squad drill and says one thing a swordsman would. His "death" in the throne room stays as the city saw it (rule 33).
- **Finadd Moroch Nevath on the bridge (Ch 7).** The squad holds beside him at the principal bridge over Main Canal.
- **Preda Unnutal Hebaz (Ch 6).** She inspects the Whitefinders before Brans Keep. She dies there, as canon has it.

## Book Six (*The Bonehunters*): the Third and the Fourth
- ★ **Paran and Dujek in the Host (Fourth's chapters).** Dujek on his feet, then the plague. Paran as the officer who takes over. The order of events is canon's.
- **Fiddler before Y'Ghatan (Third, Ch 3).** A word at the siege lines. The fire and the escape stay canon's.
- **Lostara Yil (Third, Ch 1).** On the Odhan, about Leoman's trail. Rule 21 holds: the pursuit is never finished.

## Book Seven (*Reaper's Gale*): the Third and the Letherii
- ★ **Keneb and the marines inland (Third, Ch 3–5).** Keneb gives the Third its orders. Hellian's squad at the next fire.
- **Brys back in Letheras (Letherii, Ch 6).** The Letherii squad sees the Champion who was dead walk into the city. Rule 33 already closed this.
- **Hedge and Quick Ben at Letheras (Third, Ch 7).** Hedge is fresh back. The Third meets him in the street, the first time they hear of him (Book Ten's line builds on it).

## Book Eight (*Toll the Hounds*): the Fourth in Darujhistan
- ★ **K'rul's Bar before the raid (Ch 1–4).** Picker, Blend, Mallet and Antsy: old Host soldiers drinking with old Host soldiers. Mallet's death (Ch 5) then lands harder (callback 3). The raid itself stays canon's.
- ★ **Spinnock at a Kef Tanar board in Black Coral (Ch 3).** Q30's option (a) with a face on it: the playable Kef Tanar, against Spinnock, after the page check.
- **Kruppe at the Phoenix (Ch 1).** He knows what the Guild's paper says, and talks around it.

## Book Nine (*Dust of Dreams*): the Third and the Letherii
- ★ **Brys and Aranict walking the camp (Letherii, Ch 4).** Canon ch 19: each soldier needs "a stone of loyalty". The squad gives an answer to their faces.
- **Hedge's company (Third, Ch 3).** The Third works a night beside Hedge's new company. Rule 93's recipes stay closed.
- **Keneb before his ride (Third, Ch 5).** One talk the day of the torn tent. His death at the Nah'ruk is told after, as now.

## Book Ten (*The Crippled God*): all three squads
- ★ **The Fourth meets Hedge (Ch 7).** Today the Fourth only hears of him (v13.7). Under the new line the Fourth can walk up the line and see him: Brisk says it to his face. He holds the hill, as canon has it.
- **Paran on the ridge (Ch 7).** The Fourth's High Fist sends them to the Third's stretch himself.
- **Fiddler at the barrows (Epilogue).** One exchange with the captain. The speeches stay unheard (rule 110).
