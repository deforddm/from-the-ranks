Hethra

City of the Fallen

Where the Crippled God's pain fell burning from the sky, Hethra died in a night. Its ruins are thick with godshards, and every faction wants them. Lead your warband through the streets, rooftops and plank bridges, bring back what you can, and keep your fighters alive from one battle to the next.

The gameWhat is in version 0.17

Fighters with faces

Every fighter is a jointed figure with a face, armour, cloth and a helm that fits their warband. Up close, cloth shows its weave, mail its rings, leather its grain and iron its rust; faces have eyes and brows, hands have fingers, and everyone carries pouches, a knife and straps. Each warband wears its own marks: marine munitions, cult chains, Seguleh rank slashes, Barghast skull paint, Andii silver, Imass antlers and bone.

A warband that lives on

Play a campaign. After each battle the wounded roll for their fate: a scar, an old wound, a missed fight or death. Survivors gain experience and choose their advances.

The night after

After every campaign battle a night comes round your fire: a bonecaster, deserters, a fever, a cellar opened by a quake, thieves on the roofs, a cold tomb, a champion's challenge. Choose how to meet it, and often a die decides: coin, godshards, experience, new blood, or a fighter who must sit out the next fight.

The camp

Your fighters gather round a fire between battles. Sell godshards to the dealers, buy weapons and armour, hire new blood and pick your next fight. The campaign saves itself.

Seven battlefields

The Idol Square, the Broken Causeway split by a canal, the Tower Ward, where the fighting goes up ladders and across planks between towers, the Starfall, a crater round a burning lump of the Fallen god, the Sleeper's Stair, a stepped temple with the sleeping goddess on its top tier, the Drowned Quarter, where the streets stand waist deep in black water round Mael's fallen bell, and the Azath Yard, a walled yard of dead trees and barrows round one of the old houses. Wading is slow and every step leaves rings on the water, but the water breaks a fall.

Six kinds of battle

Hunt godshards, fight a street brawl to the last, hold the ring of witch-fire when night falls, take their leader, cut through their lines, or dig for a paymaster's strongbox buried under one of four marked spots and carry it home on someone's back while the other side tries to cut them down.

Weather

Half the nights are still. The rest bring driving rain, with lightning and thunder over the ruins, or a fog so thick nobody sees past 14 m. Rain makes shots harder and wet stone treacherous; fog brings every fight in close.

Sound and music

Steel on steel, crossbows, Moranth munitions, warrens tearing open, feet sloshing through flood water, dry bone crumbling, spades biting into old earth, rain on stone, thunder, wind and fire in the ruins, and a score for the title, the camp and the battle. All of it is synthesized in code.

Keeps itself current

Install it once. Each time Hethra starts it checks for a newer version, fetches it in the background and restarts into it from the title screen. You can turn that off in Settings.

Blood and dice

Each fighter has their own stat line, weapons and armour. Every roll lands on screen, and blows land hard: the view shakes, blood hits the wet stones, fighters go down.

Hidden in the dark

Line of sight is real. Enemies you cannot see stay hidden, and a figure behind a wall shows only as a silhouette.

Warrens and munitions

Mages open warrens of shadow, storm, healing, sand, pain, Darkness, the clans' dead and the Imass hearth-fire. Marines carry Moranth sharpers, crackers and the dreaded cusser.

An enemy that plays to win

The foe moves, shoots, charges, casts and contests the shards and the ring on its own, and you see its moves only when your fighters can see it.

WarbandsSeven to choose from

Malazan Marines

A squad of the Empire's marines with crossbows, Moranth munitions, a sapper and a squad mage who would rather be anywhere else.

Cult of the Chained One

Pilgrims of the fallen god, many and unafraid of wounds. Chain-priests, shard-eaters, thrown stones and the Chained One's pain.

Sworn of the Whirlwind

Desert rebels who rose in the goddess's name. Dervishes, sand archers and a seer, hunting Malazans as gladly as shards.

Masked of the Seguleh

A handful of unarmoured island swordsmen, ranked by their masks. Few, costly and terribly good: their duellists parry even a tied blow.

Children of the Dark

Tall Tiste Andii with longbows and great blades. Their mage draws on Kurald Galain to sharpen a friend's edge or blind an enemy's bow.

White Face Barghast

Hill clansmen with skull-painted faces, axes, great blades and javelins. Their bone-shaman calls the clan's dead to harden a friend or howl down the enemy.

Kin of the Dust

T'lan Imass, dead for a hundred thousand years, with flint swords and dried skin. Slow and hard to put down; their Bonecaster drops old fire on the enemy and raises the fallen. When one falls for good, it crumbles to dust.

Getting startedDownload, unzip, play

  1. Download the zip with the button above, then right-click it and choose Extract All.
  2. Open the Hethra folder and run Hethra.exe. Nothing is installed.
  3. Pick your warband, then press New campaign, or Skirmish for a single battle. Continue picks up where you left off.
  4. Slow PC? On the title screen, open Settings and choose Fast. Volumes and the window are there too.
  5. Updating? From 0.15 on the game updates itself when it starts. From an older version, extract the new zip once to a fresh folder and run that copy. Your campaigns carry over, and the version shows at the bottom of the title screen.

Windows may warn you

Hethra.exe is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC from an unrecognised app.

Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your antivirus may also scan it the first time.

Want to look inside? The full Godot 4.7 project is here too: Hethra-godot-project.zip (18 MB). Import project.godot in Godot and press F5.
